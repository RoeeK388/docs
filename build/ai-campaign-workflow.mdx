Use Jurni AI to turn a campaign brief into a page, then refine it in the editor. Start with the message, product, and next action—not a long list of design instructions.

## Before you begin

Work in the correct shop. Have the product, approved claims and offer, and any campaign images or references ready. Review your [brand settings](/getting-started/brand-settings) and [AI Knowledge](/getting-started/setup-ai-knowledge) so the brief uses your brand's approved information.

## From brief to page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new build or open the page you want to edit">
    Use the [Jurni Agent](/build/jurni-agent) for a new conversation, or open **AI** inside an existing experience's editor. Be explicit about whether you want a new page or an edit to the current one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supply the campaign context">
    Attach the relevant product, ad, copy, images, or references using the available chat attachments. Describe the audience, offer, and primary action. Label design references separately from assets that should appear on the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the first version">
    Describe the page structure and the information shoppers need to make a decision. Review the result before requesting more changes. Replace unsupported or inaccurate content rather than assuming generated copy is approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine in focused steps">
    Select a section, make one clear request, and name what must stay unchanged. Use direct editor controls for simple text changes, deletion, or reordering. Check mobile and desktop as you go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and test the purchase">
    Confirm the real product, variants, subscription choices, quantity, and cart or checkout action. A designed offer is not the same as a configured discount. Use [Products and Buy Boxes](/building-funnels/buy-box-overview) for the purchase setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save, review, and publish">
    Save the work and send the appropriate [review link](/build/share-review-links). Complete the [pre-launch checklist](/building-funnels/qa-checklist), publish to the intended destination, then test the campaign entry URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to put in the first message

```text
Build a page for [product] and [audience], arriving from [ad/channel].
Use the attached approved copy and product photos.
The main action is [action].
Explain [key questions/benefits] and include [needed sections].
Follow our brand guidance. Do not invent claims, reviews, or offers.
```

Use [Prompt examples](/build/prompt-library) for common campaign and editing tasks, or [Prompting best practices](/building-funnels/ai-chat-prompts) for more guidance.

## AI, the editor, and publishing have different jobs

AI helps create and change content. The editor gives you direct control over sections and settings. Publishing makes the intended version available to shoppers. A completed chat message does not mean a campaign is live or that an experiment has started.

Check whether a change affects one page, a [Global Section](/build/global-sections), or [shared product information](/products/editing-scope) before applying it to a live campaign.

## When an edit misses the mark

Keep the parts that are right and describe the remaining mismatch. A focused screenshot and a sentence about the problem are often more useful than repeating the original brief. For an unrelated task, start a new chat and supply its context. [Chat history](/build/chat-history) helps revisit discussions; it is not page version history.

Before sending traffic, confirm the live page, purchasing flow, and tracking. AI-generated content still needs the same review as work created manually.
