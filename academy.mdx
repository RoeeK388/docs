# Jurni Academy

Build better campaign experiences with Jurni—not by memorizing features, but by learning the workflows strong growth teams use every day.

Jurni Academy is different from the Help Center. The **Help Center** answers a specific product question. **Academy** teaches you how to use Jurni to accomplish a marketing goal from start to finish.

## Learn the Jurni growth loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with the campaign">
    Define the traffic source, audience, promise, product, offer, and action you want the visitor to take. A landing page should continue the conversation that started in the ad—not restart it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give Jurni the right context">
    Attach the real product, ad creative, approved assets, Figma frames, URLs, or files that should guide the build. Keep evergreen brand and product context in AI Knowledge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build and refine with AI">
    Use a clear brief for the first build, then make focused edits. Preserve working products, purchase behavior, links, and approved claims as you iterate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch through the right path">
    Publish to the destination that fits your setup, create or use the appropriate Smart Link, and test the complete shopper journey before sending paid traffic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test a clear hypothesis">
    Create controlled variations, split traffic through an experiment, and compare the metric that matches your hypothesis.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the result into the next iteration">
    Keep what worked, identify the next constraint in the journey, and create the next focused test instead of rebuilding the entire page after every result.
  </Step>
</Steps>
