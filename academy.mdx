Jurni Academy is the practical learning hub for marketers building and optimizing campaigns with Jurni. It is organized around **outcomes**, not product menus.

Use the Help Center when you need a specific setup answer. Use Academy when you want to learn a repeatable workflow, see how the pieces fit together, and leave with something you can actually launch.

## How Academy lessons work

Every Academy playbook follows the same structure so your team can move quickly:

1. **When to use it** — the campaign or problem the playbook is designed for.
2. **What you need** — products, ad creative, approved claims, existing pages, or other inputs to gather first.
3. **The workflow** — the sequence to follow in Jurni.
4. **Copy-ready prompts** — prompts you can paste into Jurni AI and adapt.
5. **What good looks like** — practical review criteria before you move on.
6. **Common mistakes** — the failure modes that usually create generic pages or broken commerce flows.
7. **Launch / test checklist** — what to verify on the actual customer journey.
8. **Next move** — the next playbook or experiment to run after the first version is live.

This mirrors how strong growth teams work: **brief → build → verify → launch → learn → iterate**.

## Popular learning paths

### Paid social to landing page

Start with [Turn an ad into a landing page](/academy/playbooks/ad-to-landing-page). You will learn how to carry the same angle, audience, visual hierarchy, and offer from the ad into the page instead of sending paid traffic to a generic destination.

Then use [Optimize above the fold](/academy/playbooks/optimize-above-fold) to tighten the part of the page every visitor sees first.

### Build a new page type

Choose the structure that matches the campaign:

- [Product launch landing page](/academy/playbooks/product-launch)
- [Advertorial](/academy/playbooks/advertorial)
- [Listicle](/academy/playbooks/listicle)
- [Founder story](/academy/playbooks/founder-story)
- [UGC / creator landing page](/academy/playbooks/creator-page)

Each playbook teaches the strategy behind the page type instead of asking AI to “make it convert.”

### Build, test, improve

Use [Create a controlled A/B test variation](/academy/playbooks/controlled-variation) to change one meaningful variable while preserving the rest of the experience. Then follow [Run your first Jurni experiment](/academy/playbooks/first-experiment) and [Turn results into your next test](/academy/playbooks/results-to-next-test).

## AI is a workflow, not a one-shot generator

The most effective Jurni workflow is rarely one giant prompt. Strong teams use AI like a creative teammate:

**give context → establish direction → review → make focused changes → verify commerce → publish → test**.

Start with [Prompting 101](/academy/prompting-101). It teaches the Jurni **BRIEF** framework for writing useful prompts and explains when a short conversational request is better than a long structured brief.

## Videos and walkthroughs

Academy is built so each written playbook can also include a short recorded walkthrough. Until a lesson has an approved recording, the written workflow remains complete on its own—there are no fake or placeholder tutorials required to understand the process.

Browse [Videos & walkthroughs](/academy/videos) to see the recording plan and the lessons that should be prioritized first.
