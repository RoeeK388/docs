**SmartLinks** let you place a single URL in your ad and change the destination behind it anytime in Jurni, without ever editing the live link itself and potentially resetting an ad into the learning phase. When you're ready to test a new agentic shopping journey against a live ad, you update the destination in Jurni. The link in your ad stays exactly the same.

## What Is a SmartLink

A SmartLink is a single URL that you place in your ad. The destination behind that link can be changed anytime within Jurni, without ever editing the live link itself.

## Why It Matters

Every time you change a link on Meta, the algorithm resets. Your ad drops back into the learning phase, and you lose the optimization Meta already built for that audience.

SmartLinks solve this. The URL in your ad never changes, so Meta never resets. Only the destination behind it changes.

**In short:** one link, set once. Swap what's behind it as often as you need.

## How to Set Up a SmartLink

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a SmartLink">
    From your Jurni dashboard, create a new SmartLink.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the destination">
    Set the destination it should point to first. You have two options:
    - **An agentic shopping journey**, built and generated in Jurni
    - **A custom URL**, pointing to an existing page you've already built
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the SmartLink">
    Copy the SmartLink and place it in your ad on Meta (or any entry source).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the ad">
    Publish the ad as usual. Meta only ever sees the SmartLink, never the destination behind it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing the Destination Later

Once your SmartLink is live in an ad, you can swap the destination whenever you want:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SmartLink">
    Open the SmartLink in Jurni.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a new destination">
    Select the new destination: another agentic shopping journey or a different custom URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save. The change takes effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
If a new agentic shopping journey is clearly outperforming the one currently live, swap the destination in Jurni. Don't touch the ad on Meta.
</Tip>

## SmartLink vs. Changing the Ad Directly

|                | Change the destination in Jurni | Change the URL on Meta            |
| -------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Learning phase | Unaffected                      | Resets                            |
| Speed          | Immediate                       | Requires ad edit and re-approval  |
| Effort         | A few clicks in Jurni           | Rebuilding or editing the ad itself |

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a new SmartLink for every campaign?">
    No. One SmartLink can stay live in an ad indefinitely. You're only ever changing what it points to.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will Meta see any change when I swap the destination?">
    No. The link Meta reads never changes. Only the destination it resolves to changes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
