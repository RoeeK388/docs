Connecting Shopify Analytics lets your Jurni funnel data flow into your Shopify analytics dashboards. Because Jurni runs as a Headless storefront on your store, all traffic and sales it drives are attributed to the Jurni channel, so you can see Jurni's contribution to your store performance alongside the rest of your sales channels in Shopify.

To set this up, you'll install Shopify's Headless channel, create a storefront for Jurni, copy its public access token, and paste it into Jurni's Data Integrations settings.

The full setup takes about 3 minutes.

## Before You Start

You'll need:

- A Shopify store with admin access (you must have the **Apps and channels** permission)
- A Jurni account connected to the same Shopify store

## Step 1 — Install the Headless channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Headless channel">
    Go to the Headless channel on the Shopify App Store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the app">
    Click **Add app**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the install">
    Approve the install in your Shopify admin.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Headless channel is a free, official Shopify app. It's the standard way to create and manage Storefront API access for any custom storefront, including Jurni.

## Step 2 — Create your Jurni storefront

After the channel is installed:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Headless">
    In your Shopify admin sidebar, under **Sales channels**, click **Headless**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a storefront">
    Click **Create storefront**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm defaults">
    Shopify will create a new storefront with default access tokens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3 — Rename the storefront to Jurni

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the storefront">
    On the **Storefronts** page, select the storefront you just created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the name">
    Beside **Storefront name**, click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rename">
    Rename it to `Jurni Headless Storefront`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
**Why the name matters:** Sales attributed to this storefront will appear in your Shopify analytics under this name. Naming it clearly makes it easy to see Jurni's contribution to your channel performance later on.
</Tip>

## Step 4 — Copy the public access token

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find Storefront API tokens">
    Still on your storefront's page, find the **Storefront API tokens** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage tokens">
    Click **Manage** (or **Manage Storefront API**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the public token">
    Copy the **Public access token**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
The Headless channel shows both a **public** and a **private** access token. Jurni uses the **public** token, copy that one. Treat the private token as a secret and don't share it.
</Warning>

## Step 5 — Paste the token into Jurni

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data Integrations">
    In Jurni, go to **Settings → Data Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Shopify Analytics">
    Find the **Shopify Analytics** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the token">
    Paste your public access token into the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the token is valid, the card will switch to **Connected** and your Jurni funnel data will start syncing into your Shopify analytics within a few minutes.

## Verifying It's Working

Once connected, you can confirm everything is flowing in your Shopify admin:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reports">
    Go to **Analytics → Reports**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a channel report">
    Open any **Sales by channel** or **Sessions by channel** report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look for Jurni">
    Look for `Jurni Headless Storefront` in the channel breakdown. Once Jurni-driven traffic and sales come in, they'll appear there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
It can take a few hours for the first data points to show up after connecting.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

**"This token isn't valid"**
Double-check that you copied the full token without extra spaces. Make sure you copied the **public** access token, not the private one.

**I don't see the Headless channel in my admin**
The channel was installed but isn't pinned. Use the search field at the top of your Shopify admin and search for `Headless` to find it.

**I don't see Jurni traffic in Shopify analytics yet**
Data takes a few hours to appear after the first connection. If it's been more than 24 hours and you're still not seeing anything, reach out to support.

**I want to use a different name for the storefront**
That's fine. Any name works, but we recommend keeping `Jurni` in the name so it's easy to recognize in your analytics.

## Keeping Your Access Secure

- The **public** access token is meant to be used in browsers and is safe to share with Jurni
- The **private** access token should never be shared, keep it secret
- If you ever need to rotate the token, you can do it from the **Headless** channel under **Storefront API tokens → Rotate token**
