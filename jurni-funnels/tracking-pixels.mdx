Tracking pixels allow you to measure funnel performance, conversions, and user behavior across your Jurni funnels. Jurni supports Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Blotout, and Triple Whale, with additional custom tracking available on request. Once configured, tracking is automatically loaded on your funnel pages.

## Accessing Tracking Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Log in to your Jurni account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Go to **Settings** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Data Integrations">
    Click **Data Integrations**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure Tracking Pixels

In **Tracking Pixels**, enter the IDs for the platforms you use.

### Supported tracking platforms

- **Meta Pixel ID** — Tracks conversions and performance for Meta Ads.
- **Google Tag Manager ID (GTM-XXXXXXX)** — Enables advanced event tracking and tag management.
- **Google Analytics ID (G-XXXXXXX)** — Tracks sessions and funnel performance in GA4.
- **Blotout ID** — Enables privacy-first tracking and data governance.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Paste your IDs">
    Paste the relevant ID into each field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Tracking pixels are automatically injected into all funnel pages. No manual code installation is required.

## Triple Whale Integration

Jurni includes a native integration with **Triple Whale** for advanced attribution and analytics.

### How it works

- Toggle **Triple Whale** on in the Data Integrations section
- Jurni automatically loads the Triple Whale script on all funnel pages
- No additional configuration is required inside Jurni

Once enabled, Triple Whale begins tracking funnel traffic and conversions using your existing Triple Whale setup.
