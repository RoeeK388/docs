Jurni allows you to invite team members to your workspace and assign roles based on the level of access they need. This makes it easy to collaborate while keeping sensitive settings secure.

## Accessing Team Members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Log in to your Jurni account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Go to **Settings** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Members">
    Click **Members**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You'll see a list of all current team members and pending invitations.

## Inviting a New Team Member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Invite Member">
    Click **Invite Member** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter email">
    Enter the team member's email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a role">
    Select a role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Send the invitation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The invited user will receive an email with instructions to join your workspace.

## Team Roles and Permissions

Jurni currently supports two roles:

### Admin (Owner)

Admins have full access to the workspace, including:

- All product features
- Billing and subscription settings
- Inviting and managing team members
- Workspace and account-level settings

### Staff

Staff members have access to day-to-day product work, including:

- Creating and editing funnels
- Running experiments
- Viewing analytics and performance data

Staff **cannot**:

- Access billing or subscription settings
- Invite or remove users
- Change account-level permissions

This ensures collaboration without exposing sensitive controls.

## Pending Invitations

Invited users who haven't accepted yet will appear as **Pending Invitation** in the members list.

From here, you can:

- See which invitations are still pending
- Re-invite users if needed
