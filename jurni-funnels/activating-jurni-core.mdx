If you're using Shopify, activating **Jurni Core** is a required step during onboarding. Jurni Core enables tracking, analytics, and attribution between your Shopify store and Jurni funnels. Without Jurni Core enabled, Jurni cannot accurately track conversions or attribute performance.

## When You Need to Do This

Activating Jurni Core is required if:

- Your store is on Shopify
- You want accurate funnel analytics and attribution
- You plan to launch funnels, run experiments, or send paid traffic

This step must be completed once per Shopify store as part of getting set up properly.

## Where to Activate Jurni Core

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Log in to your Jurni account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Go to **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Account">
    Click **Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Activate Jurni Core">
    Find **Activate Jurni Core**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Activate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Activate & Open">
    Click **Activate & Open**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Shopify admin">
    You'll be redirected to your Shopify admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the app">
    Enable the **Jurni Core** app in your Shopify theme editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Save changes in Shopify.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once enabled, you'll be redirected back to Jurni and the status will update to **Active**.

## What Jurni Core Enables

When Jurni Core is active, Jurni can:

- Track sessions and funnel traffic accurately
- Attribute purchases to funnels and campaigns
- Power analytics, experiments, and reporting
- Connect Shopify checkout events back to Jurni

Without this step, tracking and attribution will be incomplete.

## Troubleshooting

**Jurni Core still shows as Disabled**

- Confirm the app is enabled in your active Shopify theme
- Make sure changes were saved in the theme editor

If the issue persists, contact [support@getjurni.ai](mailto:support@getjurni.ai).

## Next Steps

After activating Jurni Core, continue onboarding by:

- Installing tracking pixels
