Sections are added to a funnel using the **Section Library**, located in the left-side panel of the Funnel Editor. Every section you add comes from this library and is inserted directly into the funnel canvas. This article explains exactly how adding sections works in Jurni.

## Where Sections Are Added From

All sections are added from the **Section Library**. To access it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a funnel">
    Open a funnel in the Funnel Editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the left-side panel">
    Look at the left-side panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Sections">
    Click the **Sections** tab to open the Section Library.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Section Library opens on the left side of the editor.

## Using the Section Library

The Section Library contains a wide variety of pre-built sections you can use to construct your funnel.

Sections are organized into categories and layouts, making it easy to find the right structure for your funnel. Many section types are available in the library, and new ones are added over time.

## Adding a Section to the Funnel

To add a section:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Section Library">
    Open the Section Library from the left-side panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse or search">
    Browse or search for a section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select one or more">
    Select one or more sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Sections">
    Click **Add Sections**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The selected section or sections are immediately added to the funnel canvas.

## Where New Sections Appear

New sections are added:

- Below the currently selected section, or
- At the bottom of the funnel if no section is selected

You can reposition sections after adding them.

## Selecting a Section After Adding

Once a section is added:

- Click the section directly on the canvas, or
- Select it from the section list

Selecting a section opens its properties in the right-side editing panel.

## Important Notes

- Sections can only be added through the Section Library
- Multiple sections can be added at once
- Saving changes to funnels is not automatic; you must save your funnel draft or update your published funnel to save changes
