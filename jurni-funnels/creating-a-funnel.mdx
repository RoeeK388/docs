Learn the different ways to create a funnel in Jurni, using AI, starting from a template, or building from scratch. Jurni offers three ways to create a funnel: generate one with AI in minutes, start with a conversion-optimized template, or build from scratch with complete creative control. This article walks you through each method.

## Starting a New Funnel

There are two ways to access funnel creation:

- **Quick Action (anywhere in Jurni):** Click **Create New Funnel** below the Jurni icon in the top left corner.
- **From Funnels page:**
  1. Click **Funnels** in the left sidebar
  2. Click **Create New Funnel** in the top right

You'll see three options for creating your funnel.

## Option 1: Generate with AI

The fastest way to create a complete, on-brand funnel using simple text prompts.

### How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Generate with AI">
    Click **Generate with AI**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a prompt">
    Write a prompt describing your funnel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Generate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for AI">
    Wait 2 to 5 minutes while AI builds your funnel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and customize">
    Review and customize the result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### When to use AI

- **Speed** — Need a funnel fast (under 5 minutes)
- **Product-based** — Creating funnels for specific products
- **Starting point** — Want a foundation to customize
- **Brand consistency** — AI uses your brand settings automatically

### Writing effective prompts

Be specific about:

- Product name or type
- Funnel purpose (launch, sale, upsell)
- Target audience
- Key messages or benefits
- Required sections

**Example prompt:** "Create a landing page for our new protein powder targeting fitness enthusiasts. Highlight the 30g protein per serving, 5 flavors available, and include customer testimonials. Add a limited-time 25% off offer."

## Option 2: Start from Template

Choose from pre-built, conversion-optimized templates designed for different funnel types.

### How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Start from Template">
    Click **Start from Template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the library">
    Browse the template library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    Preview templates by clicking on them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Template">
    Click **Use Template** on your chosen design.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize">
    Customize the template with your content.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Template categories

Jurni templates are grouped by funnel format, making it easy to choose the right structure based on your campaign and traffic source.

1. **Creator** — Templates designed for creator and influencer-led funnels. Built to feel personal and native, using creator content, testimonials, and social proof to drive trust and conversion.
2. **Listicle** — Editorial-style templates that present products through lists, comparisons, or "best of" formats. Ideal for discovery, education, and high-intent browsing traffic.
3. **Product** — Product-focused templates centered around a single product or offer. Designed to highlight benefits, features, reviews, and a clear call to action.
4. **Advertorial** — Long-form, story-driven templates that blend education and persuasion. Best suited for paid traffic where warming the user before conversion is critical.
5. **Gifting** — Templates built for gift-focused flows, bundles, and special occasions. Optimized for seasonal gifting, promotions, and multi-product offers.

<Note>
**More templates and categories coming soon.** We're continuously expanding the template library to support additional funnel types and use cases.
</Note>

### When to use templates

- **Proven layouts** — Start with tested, high-converting designs
- **Specific use case** — Need a funnel type that matches a template
- **Design inspiration** — Want to see what works
- **Faster than scratch** — Quicker than blank canvas but more control than AI

## Option 3: Build from Scratch

Start with a blank canvas and build your funnel exactly how you want it.

### How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Build from Scratch">
    Click **Build from Scratch**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start with a blank page">
    Start with a blank page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add sections">
    Add sections from the section library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize">
    Customize every element.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build page by page">
    Build your funnel page by page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### When to build from scratch

- **Unique design** — Need something specific that templates don't offer
- **Complete control** — Want to design every detail yourself
- **Brand-specific layouts** — Following internal design guidelines
- **Learning** — Want to understand how sections work together

### Building process

1. **Add sections** — Click **+** to browse the section library
2. **Arrange layout** — Drag sections to reorder
3. **Customize content** — Edit text, images, and styling
4. **Add products** — Connect Shopify products where needed
5. **Preview and refine** — Test on desktop and mobile

## Naming Your Funnel

After selecting your creation method, give your funnel a clear, descriptive name.

**Good funnel names:**

- "Black Friday Landing Page - Collagen"
- "Q1 Product Launch - Yoga Mat"
- "Meta Ads - Summer Sale 2025"

**Why naming matters:**

- Easy to find in your funnel list
- Clear for team members
- Helps with organization and filtering

## Adding Tags

Organize your funnels with tags for easy filtering.

**Common tags:**

- Campaign type: "Product Launch", "Seasonal", "Evergreen"
- Traffic source: "Meta Ads", "Google Ads", "Email"
- Quarter: "Q1 2025", "Q4 2024"
- Product: "Skincare", "Supplements", "Apparel"

Add tags when creating the funnel or anytime from the funnel settings.

## What Happens Next?

After creating your funnel:

**AI-Generated Funnels:**

- Review the AI-generated pages and sections
- Edit content and customize design
- Add or remove sections as needed
- Preview and publish

**Template-Based Funnels:**

- Replace placeholder content with your own
- Swap images and update copy
- Adjust colors and styling
- Connect your products

**Built from Scratch:**

- Start adding sections from the library
- Build your page structure
- Add content and products
- Style and customize

## Tips for Creating Great Funnels

<Tip>
**Start simple.** Your first funnel doesn't need to be complex. A single landing page with a clear offer can be highly effective.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Use your brand settings.** Make sure your brand settings are configured first. AI and templates will use them automatically.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Mobile-first mindset.** Always preview on mobile. Most traffic comes from mobile devices.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Test multiple approaches.** Try creating the same funnel with AI vs. template vs. scratch to see which workflow you prefer.
</Tip>

## What's Next?

Now that you've created your funnel:

1. **Learn the funnel editor** — Understand how to edit and customize
2. **Add sections** — Browse the section library
3. **Connect products** — Add Shopify products to your pages
4. **Preview and publish** — Test and make your funnel live
