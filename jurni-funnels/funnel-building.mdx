# Funnel Building

Create, name, and publish agentic shopping journeys with AI or a template in minutes.

## What is a funnel?

In Jurni, a funnel is a single-page agentic shopping journey built around one specific goal, whether that is converting a paid ad click, launching a creator campaign, or capturing email sign-ups. Unlike a static product page, each journey is matched to the shopper's entry source and intent.

Every funnel lives in the **Funnels** section of your dashboard and moves through **Draft, Public, and Live** as you take it from creation to a connected campaign.

## Building Your Funnel

Start from a template or generate one with AI, add products and media, and publish when ready.

### Create with AI

The AI builder generates a structured agentic shopping journey from your prompt.

1. On the Create a Funnel page, click **Generate with AI**.
2. Give Jurni as much relevant context as possible.
3. Review the plan Jurni creates before building.
4. Refine the brief if needed.
5. Confirm to proceed and let Jurni build the journey.

Useful context can include:
- Existing products, media, or templates
- Connected Meta Ads
- A Figma file
- Uploaded files or images
- A URL to an existing page or reference

More detail generally produces a more precise result.

### Create from a Template

1. Click **Create Funnel**.
2. Choose **Start with Template**.
3. Name the funnel.
4. Choose the product or products.
5. Upload or select media.
6. Choose a template from Marketplace or **My Templates**.
7. Click **Create**.

The funnel opens directly in the editor.

## Publish Your Funnel

When the funnel is ready, click **Publish Funnel** in the top-right corner of the editor.

You will be prompted to set:
- An internal account name for your dashboard
- A URL slug for the shareable link

Once published, the funnel moves from **Draft** to **Public** status.

## Funnel Statuses

**Draft** — Still in progress and not externally accessible.

**Public** — A URL slug has been created and anyone with the link can access the page.

**Live** — A Meta ad is actively connected to the funnel and traffic is flowing from a running campaign.

## Rename Your Funnel

Open a funnel and click the edit icon next to its name in the top-left corner.

## Duplicate Your Funnel

On the Funnels page, hover over a funnel, open the three-dot menu, and click **Duplicate**. The copy is created in Draft status and labeled as a duplicate.

## Archive Your Funnel

Open the three-dot menu and click **Archive**. Archived funnels move to the **Archived** tab and can be reactivated later.

### Why no delete?

Jurni does not permanently delete funnels. Archiving preserves your work, templates, and historical data.
