The **Funnel Editor** is where you customize every part of a funnel in Jurni. It's designed to make building and iterating on funnels fast, visual, and flexible, without requiring code. This article explains how the Funnel Editor is structured and how to navigate it. Detailed editing actions are covered in separate articles within the Building Funnels collection.

## Accessing the Funnel Editor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Funnels">
    Go to **Funnels**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a funnel">
    Open an existing funnel or create a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Editor opens">
    You'll be taken directly into the Funnel Editor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Funnel Editor Layout

The Funnel Editor is organized into three main areas.

### Canvas (Center)

The canvas shows a live preview of your funnel. This is where you:

- View the full funnel layout
- Select sections and elements
- See changes update in real time

What you see on the canvas closely reflects the live experience.

### Sections Panel (Left)

The sections panel controls the structure of your funnel. From here, you can:

- Add new sections
- Reorder sections
- Remove sections
- Select a section to edit

Each section represents a block of content, such as a hero, product section, testimonials, or CTA.

### Editing Panel (Right)

The editing panel changes based on what you select in the funnel. Depending on the selected section or element, you can:

- Edit text and copy
- Replace images or videos
- Adjust layout and spacing
- Configure products and CTAs
- Modify styling and design options

## How Editing Works

Editing in the Funnel Editor is contextual:

- Click a section to edit its structure and layout
- Click text to update copy
- Click media to replace images or videos
- Click buttons or products to configure behavior

Changes are saved automatically as you edit.

## Funnel Status and Changes

Funnels start in **Draft** status. Edits made in Draft are not visible to users until the funnel is published.

Once published:

- You can continue editing
- Updates can be republished when ready

## What the Funnel Editor Is Used For

The Funnel Editor allows you to:

- Customize funnel layout and structure
- Adjust messaging and visuals
- Control product presentation
- Apply brand styling
- Prepare funnels for publishing and traffic

Each of these areas is covered in dedicated articles.
