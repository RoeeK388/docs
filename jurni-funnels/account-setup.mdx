This guide walks you through the core setup steps to get your account ready: your custom subdomain, tracking pixels, team members, and brand settings. Complete each section before generating your first agentic shopping journey.

- Inviting Team Members

## Custom Subdomain Setup

**Settings > Account Settings > Account**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Subdomain Setup">
    Navigate to **Settings > Account Settings > Account** and scroll to the **Subdomain Setup** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a subdomain prefix">
    Enter your preferred subdomain prefix. You can use **shop**, **get**, **buy**, or any word that fits your brand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the preview">
    A live preview of your full subdomain renders as you type. Confirm it looks right before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and add DNS records">
    Click **Save**. Jurni will display the DNS values you need; copy these and add them to your DNS provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Your subdomain status will show as **Pending** while DNS propagates. Click **Refresh** after a few minutes. Once verified, the status updates to **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
DNS propagation typically takes a few minutes but can occasionally take longer depending on your provider. The status will not update until propagation is complete.
</Note>

## Third-Party Tracking Pixels

**Settings > Integrations > Data Integrations**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data Integrations">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations** and scroll down to **Data Integrations**. You'll see the full list of pixels Jurni supports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle the pixel on">
    Find the pixel you want to activate and toggle it **on**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your ID">
    In the field that appears, enter the required **Pixel ID** or **Tag ID**. You'll find this in your platform's settings; copy it from there and paste it here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The pixel is active immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Repeat this process for each pixel you want to connect. Each integration is set up independently.

## App Integrations

**Settings > Integrations > Data Integrations**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data Integrations">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations** and scroll down to **Data Integrations**. You'll see the full list of app integrations Jurni supports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle the app on">
    Find the app you want to connect and toggle it **on**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credential">
    In the field that appears, enter the required **App Key** or credential. You'll find this in your app's settings or developer dashboard; copy it from there and paste it here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The integration is active immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Inviting Team Members

**Settings > Members**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Members">
    Go to **Settings > Members** and click **Invite Member** in the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter details">
    Enter the team member's email address and select their role from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send Invitation**. The invitee will receive an email with instructions to join.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Only users with an **Owner** or **Admin** role can invite new members. If you don't see the Invite Member button, contact your account admin.
</Note>

**Available roles:**

- **Owner:** Full control of shop and organization
- **Admin:** Manage shop and invite team members
- **Staff:** Manage shop dashboard and content
- **Designer:** Design and manage funnels

## Brand and Design System

**Settings > Brand**

Your brand settings are the foundation the engine uses when generating every agentic shopping journey. Take time to set these up accurately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Logo URL">
    Upload or paste your logo URL. This will appear across all agentic shopping journeys generated for your brand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brand Description">
    Describe your brand's unique tone and style. This helps the engine generate on-brand funnel content without additional prompting; the more specific you are, the better the output.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brand Colors">
    Click each color swatch to open the color picker. You can type in a hex code directly, or use the eyedropper tool to sample colors from your live website. Work through each token, from *surface* to *neutral*, then click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Border Radius">
    Defaults are set to: Card = Small, Button = Medium, Media = None. Adjust these to match your brand's visual style and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Typography">
    Set up both your mobile and desktop typography separately. Even if they're identical for your brand, both need to be configured. Under **Headings**, select your Google Font and adjust size, weight, text transform, line height, and letter spacing. If you're using a custom font not on Google Fonts, upload it directly. Note that uploaded fonts have fewer adjustable properties than Google Fonts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Click **Save Changes** after each section within Brand settings. Changes are not auto-saved.
</Warning>
