Saved Sections let you take a section you've customized in one funnel and reuse it across other funnels, so you don't have to rebuild the same hero, testimonial, or CTA every time.

## What Saved Sections Are

A **Saved Section** is a custom section, with your content, styling, and configuration intact, that lives in your workspace and can be dropped into any funnel from the Section Library.

Use them to:

- Reuse a proven hero or offer across campaigns
- Keep brand-consistent CTAs, footers, or announcement bars
- Ship new funnels faster by assembling from your own building blocks
