For Shopify shops, **Products → Manage products** controls the product scope available in Jurni. Use it to keep the catalog relevant to the products your team builds with.

This is different from changing the product's status or sales-channel availability in Shopify. A Jurni visibility setting does not create a product, change inventory, or publish it to a Shopify channel.

## Review your current product list

Open **Products**, search for the product, and check the list's status filters. The list distinguishes statuses such as **Active** and **Unlisted**. A product can be missing from the current list because of a filter, not because its integration failed.

Check the active shop before changing anything, especially when your account manages multiple stores.

## Set product scope

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage products">
    From the Products area in a Shopify shop, select **Manage products**. The dialog is titled **Manage Products** and lets you select visible products.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review collections, tags, and status">
    Configure the available **Collections**, **Tags**, and **Status** selections. Use the store's existing values rather than inventing a new collection or tag here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the scope">
    Select **Save scope**. The saved scope applies to product availability in Jurni's Products area, editor, and AI workflows—not just to the current search result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the product you need">
    Search again and open the product selector in the relevant experience. Confirm that the required product is available before updating a live page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use **Clear all** to clear the draft scope selections, then save when you intend to remove those scope restrictions. Use **Cancel** to leave without applying the changes in that dialog.

## Scope versus a temporary filter

| Setting | What it affects |
| --- | --- |
| Search and status filters in the product list | What you are currently browsing. |
| Saved **Manage products** scope | Which products Jurni makes available to its product, editor, and AI workflows. |
| Shopify product status, channel publication, and availability | The underlying store's product configuration and commerce availability. |
| The product selected in a Buy Box | What that particular purchasing section uses. |

Do not use catalog scope as a substitute for removing a product from a live campaign or discontinuing it in Shopify. Inspect the page and purchasing flow separately.

## A product is still missing

Check the correct shop, current list filters, saved product scope, and the product's Shopify configuration. Confirm that it is available to the required storefront/channel and that its variants are usable for the intended purchase.

For non-Shopify or headless integrations, use the product availability process defined for that implementation. The current **Manage products** control documented here is Shopify-specific.
