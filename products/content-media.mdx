Open a product in **Products** to access **Edit Product Data**. This drawer manages shared Jurni product information that compatible pages and components can use across experiences.

<Warning>
  This is not an isolated text edit on one page. Before changing shared product content, consider other experiences using that product. For a campaign-only paragraph or image, edit the local section instead.
</Warning>

## Edit product information

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the correct product">
    Open Products in the correct shop and search for the product. Check its name and image before opening the edit drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the relevant tab">
    Choose the content area you need: **Variants**, **Benefits**, **Details**, **Reviews**, **Offers**, **Cross-Sell**, or **Variant Media**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the intended fields">
    Use approved product content and the correct media. Keep product facts, customer review content, and promotional messages accurate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and inspect a consuming page">
    Save the product changes and wait for confirmation. Open an experience using that product to verify how its particular components display the updated data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each tab is for

| Tab | What to review |
| --- | --- |
| **Variants** | Product option and variant presentation/configuration exposed by the drawer. Do not confuse these controls with creating Shopify variants. |
| **Benefits** | Reusable benefit text and supporting presentation, including the icon fields offered. |
| **Details** | Supporting product descriptions and details used by compatible components. |
| **Reviews** | Product review content and summary/display fields used by the Jurni product presentation. |
| **Offers** | Product offer content. Displayed offer information is not itself proof of a working checkout discount. |
| **Cross-Sell** | Products configured as cross-sell suggestions where the consuming cart/component supports them. |
| **Variant Media** | Media associated with product variants/options for supported product presentations. |

Not every section automatically uses every field. A manually written heading, a custom image, or a custom AI-built section may have its own local value or logic.

## Product data versus provider widgets

Editing product review content in this drawer is not the same as connecting Okendo, Judge.me, or another reviews provider. A provider widget uses its integration and product mapping; connect and configure it through the [review integration workflow](/integrations/review-integrations).

Similarly, adding offer text does not create an automatic Shopify discount or a valid selling plan. Configure the underlying commerce rules in the appropriate system and verify the final cart or checkout.

## Product media versus one page's image

Change shared variant media when the product's selected option should use that media in compatible product components. Change a local image in the page editor when only that campaign's section needs a different visual.

After changing variant media, test each relevant option and check that the displayed image corresponds to what will actually be added to cart.

## Sync Product

The drawer includes **Sync Product**. Treat it as a product-refresh operation, not a way to save arbitrary local page edits or to change which catalog products are visible. Review the result and any important customizations after refreshing.

For a product missing from the library, first inspect [product visibility](/products/catalog-visibility) and the [missing-product checks](/building-funnels/sync-new-products).
