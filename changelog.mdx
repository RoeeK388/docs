Follow meaningful improvements to Jurni's marketer workflow. This page focuses on **customer-facing changes**, not internal engineering work.

<Note>
  Availability can vary by account, connected commerce setup, or publishing method. Each update links to the relevant Help Center guide when there is setup or behavior you should know about.

## September 2026

### Better gift-with-purchase quantity handling

Gift-with-purchase campaigns can now support gift quantity that increases with the quantity of matching qualifying products, while respecting the configured maximum / no-maximum behavior.

This makes product-triggered GWP campaigns more useful for offers such as “buy two qualifying items, receive two gifts” when that is the rule your team intends to run.

**What to know:** Jurni controls the supported gift qualification / cart flow, but the gift still needs the matching Shopify price or discount setup when it is promised as free.

[Set up Gift with Purchase →](/building-funnels/gift-with-purchase)

---

### Search across shops faster

The shop switcher now includes search, making it easier for teams with access to many brands / shops to jump to the correct workspace before editing, reporting, or changing settings.

[Switch between Jurni shops →](/getting-started/shop-access)

---

### Sticky CTAs can stay out of the way of the purchase section

Supported Sticky CTA Banner templates can be configured so the banner hides while the product / purchase section is visible.

This helps avoid placing a sticky conversion prompt on top of the area where the shopper is already making the purchase decision.

[Configure a Sticky CTA Banner →](/building-funnels/sticky-cta-banner)

---

### Shopify Page links are easier to access from Experiences

For experiences published to native Shopify Pages, the Shopify Page destination is now surfaced in the experience link/menu workflow, making it easier to open or copy the actual `/pages/` destination you need to QA or share.

[Publish with Shopify Pages →](/shopify-pages/setup-publish)

---

## August 2026

### CPA in Meta ad account insights

Connected Meta ad-account reporting now includes **CPA (cost per acquisition)** alongside Revenue, Clicks, Spend, and ROAS in the Ad Account Insights summary.

Use this to understand the connected ad account's advertising performance, then review the related Jurni page / experiment separately rather than treating account-level reporting as a page experiment.

[Read Meta ad account insights →](/analytics-reporting/meta-ad-insights)
