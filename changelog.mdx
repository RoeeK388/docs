# Jurni Changelog

New ways to build, review, publish, and optimize with Jurni.

This changelog highlights meaningful customer-facing product changes. Each update links to the relevant Help Center guide when more detail is available.

<Update label="August 30, 2026" tags={["Collaboration"]} description="Better page review and comments">
  Collaboration on Jurni pages is easier to use across desktop and mobile.

  ### What changed

  - Improved comment-list and popover behavior on mobile.
  - Improved the layout and responsiveness of the review experience.
  - Continued improvements to reviewer access and shared review workflows.

  ### Why it matters

  Marketing, design, and stakeholder feedback can stay attached to the page instead of being reconstructed from screenshots and Slack threads.

  **Learn more:** [Comments in Jurni](/product-updates/jurni-comments) · [Share a page for review](/build/share-review-links)
</Update>

<Update label="August 24, 2026" tags={["Commerce"]} description="Better product search for non-Shopify catalogues">
  Product search now works more reliably for supported non-Shopify and Medusa-backed catalogues.

  ### What changed

  Search can match connected products using product title, handle, description, and relevant variant information such as title, SKU, and options.

  ### Why it matters

  Teams using a connected non-Shopify catalogue can find the intended product more easily instead of browsing through the full product list.

  **Learn more:** [Supported commerce platforms](/getting-started/choose-your-setup) · [Products and variants](/building-funnels/products-and-variants)
</Update>

<Update label="August 4, 2026" tags={["Builder"]} description="Sticky sections can pin to the top or bottom">
  Sticky page sections are more flexible for campaign CTAs and persistent messages.

  ### What changed

  - Sticky position can be set to **Top** or **Bottom**.
  - Jurni added sticky banner starting layouts for common campaign use cases.
  - Page and preview rendering support the updated sticky positioning behavior.

  ### Why it matters

  You can keep a campaign CTA or important message accessible as shoppers move through a long page without building a separate one-off sticky implementation.

  **Learn more:** [Sticky CTAs](/building-funnels/sticky-cta-banner)
</Update>

<Update label="August 2, 2026" tags={["Builder"]} description="Reposition and crop images directly in the editor">
  Jurni added non-destructive image cropping to the editing workflow.

  ### What changed

  You can adjust how an image is framed inside its existing area without creating a new exported asset for every crop.

  ### Why it matters

  This is especially useful when the same source image needs a different focus on a campaign page or responsive layout.

  **Learn more:** [Image repositioning](/product-updates/image-repositioning) · [Edit text, images, and video](/build/edit-content-media)
</Update>

<Update label="April 15, 2026" tags={["AI","Builder"]} description="A new AI-first page-building workflow">
  Jurni introduced a redesigned AI chat experience for creating and refining landing experiences.

  ### What changed

  The AI workflow brought campaign context and page creation together with support for richer attachments and references, including product context, media, Figma links, and other campaign inputs available in the Jurni AI experience.

  The experience also added a page preview alongside the conversation so teams can move from the initial AI build into editing more naturally.

  ### Why it matters

  Instead of starting from an empty canvas, teams can begin with the campaign inputs they already have and use Jurni AI as the first step in the build process.

  **Learn more:** [Build with AI](/build/ai-campaign-workflow) · [Academy: Prompting 101](/academy/prompting-101) · [Prompt Library](/academy/prompt-library)
</Update>

## About this changelog

We publish updates when they materially change what a Jurni customer can build, launch, analyze, or collaborate on. Small fixes and internal infrastructure changes are omitted unless they change the customer workflow.

For deeper learning and campaign examples, visit [Jurni Academy](/academy). For exact setup steps, use the Help Center navigation or search.
