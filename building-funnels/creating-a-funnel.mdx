Jurni gives you three ways to create a funnel: generate a complete funnel with AI in minutes, start from a conversion-optimized template, or build from scratch on a blank canvas. This guide walks through each method so you can choose the one that fits your timeline and goals.

## Starting a New Funnel

You can start a new funnel from anywhere in Jurni or from the Funnels page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Quick Action">
    Click **Create New Funnel** below the Jurni icon in the top-left corner. This option is available from any page in Jurni.
  </Step>
  <Step title="From the Funnels Page">
    Click **Funnels** in the left sidebar, then click **Create New Funnel** in the top right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After you start, you will see three creation options. Pick the one that matches your needs.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Generate with AI">
    The fastest way to create a complete, on-brand funnel. Describe what you want in a text prompt and Jurni builds the funnel for you.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click Generate with AI">
        Select the AI option from the creation screen.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Write your prompt">
        Describe the funnel you want. Include your product, purpose, audience, key messages, and any sections you need.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click Generate">
        Submit your prompt to start the generation process.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Wait 2-5 minutes">
        Jurni builds your funnel pages, applies your brand settings, and structures the layout.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Review and customize">
        Open the generated funnel in the editor, adjust content, and refine the design.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      Be specific in your prompts. Include your product name or type, the funnel purpose (launch, sale, upsell), target audience, key benefits, and any required sections.
    </Tip>

    **Example prompt:**

    > Create a landing page for our new protein powder targeting fitness enthusiasts. Highlight the 30g protein per serving, 5 flavors available, and include customer testimonials. Add a limited-time 25% off offer.

    **When to use AI:**

    - You need a funnel in under 5 minutes
    - You want a product-based starting point to customize
    - You want brand consistency (AI uses your saved brand settings automatically)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Start from Template">
    Browse a library of pre-built, conversion-optimized templates designed for specific use cases and traffic types.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click Start from Template">
        Select the template option from the creation screen.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Browse the library">
        Explore templates by category to find one that matches your goal.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Preview">
        Click any template to preview how it looks and flows.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Use the template">
        Click **Use Template** to create your funnel from that layout.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Customize">
        Replace placeholder content, swap images, adjust colors, and connect your products.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **Template categories:**

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Creator" icon="user">
        Creator and influencer-led funnels with a personal, authentic tone.
      </Card>

      <Card title="Listicle" icon="list">
        Editorial layouts built around lists, comparisons, and "best of" content.
      </Card>

      <Card title="Product" icon="box">
        Single-product or single-offer focus with clear calls to action.
      </Card>

      <Card title="Advertorial" icon="newspaper">
        Long-form, story-driven pages designed for paid traffic campaigns.
      </Card>

      <Card title="Gifting" icon="gift">
        Bundles, seasonal promotions, and multi-product gift pages.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    <Note>
      More templates are added regularly. If you do not see one for your exact use case, check back soon or use AI generation.
    </Note>

    **When to use templates:**

    - You want a proven layout with conversion best practices built in
    - You have a specific use case (creator page, listicle, advertorial)
    - You want design inspiration or a faster starting point than scratch
    - You want more control over structure than AI provides
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Build from Scratch">
    A blank canvas gives you complete control over every section, layout, and design choice.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click Build from Scratch">
        Select the blank canvas option from the creation screen.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Start with a blank page">
        You begin with an empty page and full control over the structure.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add sections">
        Click the **\+** button to open the section library and add components to your page.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Arrange layout">
        Drag sections to reorder them and build the flow you want.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Customize content">
        Edit text, images, styling, and spacing for every element.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add products">
        Connect your Shopify products so visitors can purchase directly.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Preview and refine">
        Check your funnel on desktop and mobile, then adjust as needed.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **When to build from scratch:**

    - You have a unique design vision
    - You want complete control over layout and styling
    - You need brand-specific layouts that templates do not cover
    - You want to learn the Jurni editor by building piece by piece
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Naming Your Funnel

A clear funnel name keeps your dashboard organized and makes it easy for your team to understand the purpose at a glance.

**Good naming examples:**

- "Black Friday Landing Page - Collagen"
- "Q1 Product Launch - Yoga Mat"
- "Meta Ads - Summer Sale 2025"

Naming matters for finding funnels in your list, keeping team communication clear, and maintaining an organized workspace.

## Adding Tags

Tags help you organize and filter funnels. You can add tags when you create the funnel or later in settings.

**Common tag types:**

| Tag Type | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign type | Product Launch, Seasonal, Evergreen |
| Traffic source | Meta Ads, Google Ads, Email |
| Quarter | Q1 2025, Q4 2024 |
| Product | Skincare, Supplements, Apparel |

## What Happens Next

After you create a funnel, your next steps depend on the method you chose.

- **AI-generated:** Review the generated pages, edit and customize content, add or remove sections, then preview and publish.
- **Template-based:** Replace all placeholder content with your own copy, swap in your images, adjust colors to match your brand, and connect your products.
- **Built from scratch:** Continue adding sections from the library, build out your page structure, add your content and products, and apply your brand styling.

## Tips for Success

<Tip>
  Start simple. A single landing page can be highly effective. You do not need a multi-page funnel for every campaign.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Use your brand settings. AI-generated and template-based funnels pull from your saved brand settings automatically, so your colors, fonts, and tone stay consistent.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Think mobile-first. Always preview your funnel on mobile before publishing. Most visitors will see it on their phones.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Test multiple approaches. Try AI for speed, templates for proven layouts, and scratch builds for custom designs. You may find different methods work better for different campaigns.
</Tip>
