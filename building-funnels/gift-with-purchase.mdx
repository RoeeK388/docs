Gift with Purchase is a Jurni **Offer** that controls when gift products become available or are added in the Jurni cart experience.

The current offer editor supports three trigger types:

- **Minimum Amount** — trigger the gift when the cart reaches a minimum value.
- **Minimum Products** — trigger the gift when the cart contains a minimum number of products.
- **Specific Products** — trigger the gift when configured products are present in the cart.

## Choose the gift behavior

Jurni supports both single-gift and multiple-gift setups.

### Single Gift

Choose one gift product and, when needed, the exact gift variant. This is the best option when the qualifying shopper should always receive one specific item.

### Multiple Gifts

Choose multiple gift products. The current editor supports two behaviors:

- let the shopper **pick one gift** from the configured list
- enable **Auto-add all selected gifts to cart** so every eligible gift is added automatically

## Jurni vs. Shopify responsibilities

The behavior is split between Jurni and Shopify:

| Jurni | Shopify / discount system |
| --- | --- |
| Evaluates the GWP offer rules | Enforces the final price/discount in checkout |
| Adds or presents eligible gift products | Determines whether the gift is \$0, discounted, or covered by another promotion |
| Removes/manages gift state as the Jurni cart changes | Applies Shopify discount-code, automatic-discount, Function, or app logic |

<Warning>
  A product being added as a Jurni gift does **not by itself guarantee a free final price**. The current GWP editor does not provide an editable self-service control that automatically creates a new Shopify discount for the offer. Configure the price/discount behavior in Shopify or the discount system used by your store.
</Warning>

Common approaches include:

- a gift SKU priced at **\$0**
- a Shopify automatic discount
- a Shopify discount code
- a Shopify Function or third-party discount app

## Set up a GWP offer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Gift with Purchase offer">
    Open Jurni **Offers** and choose **Gift with Purchase**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the gift products">
    Configure a single gift or a list of multiple gifts. For a single gift, select the required variant when applicable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the trigger">
    Configure **Minimum Amount**, **Minimum Products**, or **Specific Products** and enter the matching rule values.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Shopify pricing">
    Confirm how the gift will become free or discounted in Shopify checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test qualification and removal">
    Test a non-qualifying cart, an exactly qualifying cart, and a cart that qualifies and then has items removed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## QA checklist

Before launch, verify:

- the correct gift product/variant is used
- the offer does not trigger below the threshold
- the offer triggers at the exact threshold
- single vs. multiple gift behavior matches the configuration
- removing qualifying items does not leave an invalid gift state
- the final gift price is correct in Shopify checkout
- other discounts stack or exclude each other as intended
- desktop and mobile cart behavior match
