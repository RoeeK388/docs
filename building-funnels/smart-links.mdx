Smart Links let you change where your ad traffic lands without ever touching the live ad URL. You place one Smart Link in your Meta ad, then update its destination inside Jurni anytime you want to test a new agentic shopping journey or redirect to a different page. Meta never sees a URL change, so your campaign stays out of Learning Phase.

## Why Smart Links matter

Every time you edit a link directly on Meta, the algorithm treats it as a significant change and resets your ad into Learning Phase. That means you lose the optimization Meta has already built, and performance drops while the system relearns.

Smart Links solve this by decoupling the visible URL from the destination. The link in your ad stays the same forever. Only the destination behind it changes. The result: one link, set once, and you can swap what is behind it as often as you need.

## How to set up a SmartLink

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a SmartLink">
    Open the Jurni dashboard and create a new SmartLink.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the destination">
    Choose where the SmartLink will send traffic:

    - An agentic shopping journey built and generated in Jurni
    - A custom URL to an existing page
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the SmartLink in your ad">
    Copy the SmartLink and paste it into your ad on Meta (or any other entry source).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Publish the ad as usual. Meta only ever sees the SmartLink, never the destination behind it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing the destination later

Once a SmartLink is live in an ad, you can swap its destination at any time without editing the ad itself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SmartLink">
    Find the SmartLink in your Jurni dashboard and open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a new destination">
    Choose another agentic shopping journey or enter a different custom URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The change takes effect immediately. Traffic from your live ad now flows to the new destination.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If a new agentic shopping journey outperforms the current one, swap the destination in Jurni. Do not touch the ad on Meta.
</Tip>

## SmartLink vs. changing the ad directly

|  | Change destination in Jurni | Change URL on Meta |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learning phase | Unaffected | Resets |
| Speed | Immediate | Requires ad edit and re-approval |
| Effort | A few clicks in Jurni | Rebuilding or editing the ad itself |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a new SmartLink for every campaign?">
    No. One SmartLink can stay live in an ad indefinitely. You are only ever changing what it points to.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will Meta see any change when I swap the destination?">
    No. The link Meta reads never changes. Only the destination it resolves to changes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
