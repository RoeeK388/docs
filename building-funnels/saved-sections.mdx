Saved sections let you build a block once and drop it into any funnel page. This keeps headers, footers, testimonials, and CTAs consistent without rebuilding them every time.

## What are saved sections?

A saved section is a reusable block of content you create inside the Jurni funnel builder. After you design a section, you can save it to your library and add it to other funnels in your workspace.

## When to use saved sections

Saved sections work best for content you repeat across pages or funnels, such as:

- Brand headers and navigation bars
- Footer blocks with links and legal text
- Testimonial or social proof sections
- Call-to-action blocks you want to keep uniform
