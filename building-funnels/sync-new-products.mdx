Jurni syncs products from your connected Shopify store automatically. You do not normally create a second copy of the product inside Jurni.

## Requirement: the product must be published to the Online Store

For a Shopify product to sync into Jurni, it needs to be available to the Shopify **Online Store** sales channel.

You can keep the product **unlisted** if you do not want shoppers discovering it through normal storefront navigation or search, but it still needs to be published to the Online Store so Jurni can access it.

<Info>
Publishing a product to the Online Store does not require you to link to it from your navigation or collections. An unlisted product can still be used in a dedicated Jurni campaign.
</Info>

## Add a new product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create or update the product in Shopify">
    Configure the title, variants, pricing, media, inventory, and any selling plans the Jurni page will need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it to Online Store">
    Make the product available to the Shopify Online Store sales channel. Use an unlisted setup if it should not be discoverable through normal storefront browsing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Jurni">
    Open the product selector in your Buy Box or commerce section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the product">
    Search by the Shopify product title and select it when it appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the commerce options">
    Confirm the expected variants, pricing, and selling plans are available before publishing the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If the product is missing

Check:

1. The correct Shopify store is connected to the Jurni workspace.
2. The product is active in Shopify.
3. The product is published to the Online Store sales channel.
4. The product has not been archived or deleted.
5. You are searching the correct product title.

If you changed product availability only moments ago, refresh the Jurni product selector and try again.

## Changes after the product is already used

When you modify variants, selling plans, or product structure in Shopify, re-open any important Jurni page using that product and test it again. Removing or replacing a Shopify variant can invalidate an old selection on a live page.
