Product Purchase Options are the controls inside a Jurni commerce section that determine **what the shopper is buying and how they want to buy it** before the purchase action is triggered.

Depending on the section and product configuration, purchase options can include:

- Product variant selection
- One-time vs. subscription purchase
- Subscription selling plan
- Quantity
- Product pricing and compare-at pricing
- Add to cart
- Direct checkout

## How the selection is resolved

Jurni uses the selected product option values to resolve the real commerce variant. This is important for products where a shopper chooses combinations such as:

- Flavor + pack size
- Size + color
- Pack size + formula
- One-time + subscription configuration

The final cart action should use the actual selected variant ID rather than relying on the text shown in the selector.

<Warning>
Always test the cart or checkout payload. A purchase section can look visually correct while still pointing to the wrong Shopify variant or selling plan.
</Warning>

## One-time purchase

For a one-time purchase, Jurni adds the selected product variant without a subscription selling plan.

Use one-time as the only option when the product is not sold as a subscription, or present it alongside subscription when shoppers should be able to choose.

## Subscription purchase

When the shopper selects subscription, Jurni uses a selling plan available for the selected product and variant.

The subscription option can affect:

- Final price
- Compare-at or savings display
- Delivery frequency
- Cart line selling-plan data

The selling plan itself is configured in Shopify or your subscription provider—not created by the Jurni section.

## Pricing

Pricing should reflect the currently selected variant, selling plan, and quantity. This matters when subscription pricing differs from one-time pricing or when variants have different prices.

Avoid hard-coding prices into a purchase section when the value should come from the connected product. Product-backed pricing is much safer when Shopify pricing changes.

## Quantity

If the section exposes a quantity selector, the selected quantity is passed into the cart or checkout action.

Quantity is different from a product pack-size variant. For example:

- **2 Pack** may be a Shopify variant.
- **Quantity 2** means adding two units of the selected variant.

Make sure the page makes that distinction clear to shoppers.

## Add to cart vs. direct checkout

A purchase CTA can follow two common patterns:

### Add to cart

Use add to cart when the shopper should remain in the store flow, open the cart drawer, add more products, or review the cart before checkout.

### Direct checkout

Use direct checkout when the experience is intentionally designed to take the selected configuration straight into checkout.

In both cases, the commerce action should carry the selected:

- Variant
- Selling plan when subscription is selected
- Quantity

## When the available options change

Review the Jurni purchase section after changes to:

- Shopify variants
- Product option names or values
- Subscription selling plans
- Product availability
- Pricing
- Pack-size structure

A live page should not depend on a variant or plan that has been removed from the connected product.

## QA checklist

Before publishing, test every meaningful purchase path:

1. Default product option.
2. Each important variant combination.
3. One-time purchase.
4. Every subscription option you intend to expose.
5. Quantity changes.
6. Add-to-cart behavior.
7. Direct-checkout behavior when used.
8. Final cart or checkout price.
