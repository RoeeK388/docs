Purchase options determine **what the shopper buys and how they buy it**. Configure the actual product selection and behavior, not only the labels and selection-card design.

The available controls depend on the section. The standard library Buy Box, an older Product Purchase Options component, and a custom AI-built Buy Box may not expose the same fields.

## Start with the selected product

Choose the real product and check the variant choices it provides. If shoppers select size and flavor, the final combination must resolve to the intended purchasable variant.

Do not treat product variants and subscription plans as the same option. A variant identifies the item; a selling plan defines its subscription purchase. Quantity is another separate value.

## One-time and subscription settings

Open the editor's **Settings → Subscriptions** to review the experience's supported subscription visibility and default-selection configuration.

The configuration distinguishes offering subscriptions alongside one-time purchases, offering subscriptions only, and offering one-time purchases only. When both purchase types are offered, the default choices include:

| Default | Intended behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Subscription first** | Preselect the first available subscription plan. |
| **One-time first** | Start with a one-time purchase unless the shopper chooses subscription. |
| **No default selection** | Require the shopper to choose their purchase type. |

Save the settings in that control and test the actual purchasing component. Do not assume every custom Buy Box consumes the same experience-level defaults; inspect its own controls and behavior too.

<Warning>
  These settings do not create subscription plans. The selected product needs valid plans from Shopify or the subscription provider. A designed subscription card cannot substitute for a selling plan that reaches checkout.
</Warning>

## Pricing and quantity

Check that the displayed amount follows the selected variant, purchase type, and quantity. Subscription pricing can differ from one-time pricing. A static price typed into a section may not change with the shopper's selection.

If the component exposes quantity, confirm how it is passed into the purchase. A “3-pack” variant at quantity 1 is not the same configuration as adding three units of another variant.

Configure discount enforcement separately from promotional copy. A compare-at price, savings badge, or “free gift” message does not by itself create the corresponding checkout discount.

## Choose the next action

Set the supported action to **Add to cart**, **Go to checkout**, or the intended external destination. On the standard library Buy Box, this is the **Action type** field.

For add to cart, confirm the correct item is added and the intended cart behavior follows. For direct checkout, inspect the resulting checkout. For an external URL, confirm where the purchase will happen and how its measurement is implemented.

## Variant-selection modal

When your page uses Jurni's supported variant-selection modal, **Settings → Variant Select Modal** offers **Default** and **Full Screen** styles. Full Screen also offers **Show product gallery**.

These are presentation settings for that modal, not a command to replace every custom selector. See [Page settings](/build/page-settings).

## Test the complete selection matrix

Test the default state, each important variant combination, one-time purchase, each subscription choice, and quantity changes where available. Check that unavailable options are handled appropriately.

For every important path, confirm the actual product, variant, quantity, selling plan, final amount, and applied discount or gift in cart or checkout. Repeat on mobile and desktop and on the real publishing destination.

If something is wrong, identify whether the problem is the source product data, section configuration, custom selection logic, or cart integration before rebuilding the design.
