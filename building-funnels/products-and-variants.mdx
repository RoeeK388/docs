A **product** is the item in your connected commerce catalogue. A **variant** is a purchasable option of that product, such as a size or flavor. A subscription **selling plan** is another part of the purchase—not a different name for a variant.

## Find a product in Jurni

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Products">
    Check that you are in the correct shop, then open **Products**. Search by the product name and review the available status filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check catalogue access">
    For Shopify, **Manage products** controls the catalogue available to the product list, editor, and AI. If a product is missing, check that scope as well as the product's Shopify availability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Edit Product Data">
    Open the product to manage its Jurni content and presentation. This drawer is not the Shopify screen for creating products, changing inventory, or setting a price.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Catalogue visibility](/products/catalog-visibility) for the difference between browsing filters and saved shop-wide scope.

## What to edit where

| Change | Where to make it |
| --- | --- |
| Create a Shopify product or variant; change price, inventory, or selling plans | Shopify and the relevant commerce/subscription app. |
| Maintain reusable benefits, detail text, curated reviews, or variant media | **Products → Edit Product Data** in Jurni. |
| Change a page's product selection, purchasing action, or layout | The Buy Box or relevant element in the Jurni editor. |
| Configure a promotion's qualifying conditions | **Offers** for the supported Shopify flow, with the actual discount configured in Shopify. |

For headless and non-Shopify stores, the connected catalogue and purchase rules come from the agreed commerce integration. Contact the team when the imported data or purchasing behavior needs changing.

## Variants versus linked products

The **Variants** tab in Edit Product Data includes presentation options such as swatches. It can also hold linked-product options used by supported components. A flavor stored as a separate product is not automatically a variant of the currently selected product.

Changing a swatch label or color in Jurni does not create a new Shopify variant. Test that the visible selection still purchases the correct real item.

## Connect the product to a page

Use [Add a Buy Box](/products/add-buy-box) to place a purchasing section and select the actual product. Review its variants and purchase types in preview, then verify the selected product, variant, quantity, and plan in the final cart or checkout.

A product title typed into a heading does not connect a product. Copying a page or switching a Buy Box's product also requires checking any separately configured review SKU, gift, or link.
