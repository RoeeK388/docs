Use **Products** to find the catalog items available to your current Jurni shop. Use the product selector inside a Buy Box or other product component to decide which item that section uses.

Shopify has a native product connection. For headless or non-Shopify stores, the available product data depends on the commerce implementation configured for the shop.

## Find a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the shop">
    Confirm that you are working in the intended brand or regional store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Products">
    Search using **Search products...** and inspect the product name and image.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review filters and visibility">
    Check the current status filters. For Shopify, also inspect **Manage products** if a saved catalog scope might exclude the item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the relevant configuration">
    Open the product to edit its shared Jurni content, or return to the page editor and select it in the intended product component.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The product edit drawer is not the same as the page editor. See [Product content and media](/products/content-media) before changing information used across experiences.

## Product, variant, selling plan, and quantity

| Item | Example | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product** | A body mist | The catalog item used by the component. |
| **Variant** | Size: 8 oz; scent: Vanilla | The purchasable option combination that reaches cart. |
| **Selling plan** | Subscription delivered every month | The subscription purchase terms attached to the selected item. |
| **Quantity** | Two units | How many units of the selected variant are added. |

A pack-size variant and quantity are not interchangeable. “2-pack, quantity 1” is different from “single item, quantity 2,” even when the shopper receives a similar number of individual items.

For Shopify, cart actions need the real selected variant and selling plan where applicable. A visible label or product image is not proof that those identifiers are correct.

## Select a product on a page

Select the Buy Box or product component and use the **Product** control it exposes. Check the resulting variants, pricing, media, and action in Preview, then test the final published destination.

For a custom purchasing component, its configuration and logic may differ from the standard library Buy Box. Do not change only the displayed name when the goal is to sell a different product.

[Add and configure a Buy Box](/products/add-buy-box)

## Product data can change outside the editor

When variants, availability, selling plans, or prices change in the commerce platform, recheck the pages that use the product. Deleting or replacing a variant can invalidate an existing selection or custom configuration.

Keep commerce source data separate from static page copy. A manually typed price or promotion can become outdated even when the connected catalog is correct.

## A product or option is missing

Check the shop, product-list filters, saved Shopify product scope, channel/storefront availability, and the component's own configuration. A missing selling plan requires a different check from a missing product.

Use [Product visibility](/products/catalog-visibility) and [Sync or find missing Shopify products](/building-funnels/sync-new-products) before recreating the Buy Box.
