## The quick rule

Tell Jurni:

1. **What you're trying to accomplish**
2. **What source material to use**
3. **What should change**
4. **What must stay the same**
5. **What not to invent**

Example:

```text
Update only the selected hero for cold Meta traffic.
Use the attached ad for the campaign angle and the attached product for product information.

Make the headline more benefit-led, shorten the paragraph, and move the approved proof below the CTA.

Keep the current product, CTA action, offer, mobile/desktop behavior outside this hero, and every section below it unchanged.
Do not invent reviews, claims, pricing, discounts, or urgency.
```

## For a new page, give campaign context

A new build needs more context than a small edit.

Useful inputs:

- audience;
- traffic source / ad;
- campaign angle;
- connected product;
- approved offer;
- primary action;
- real proof / reviews;
- reference screenshots, URLs, or Figma frames;
- what must not change / be invented.

Use [Launch your first Jurni campaign](/academy/first-campaign) for the complete workflow.

## Label your references

Do not make Jurni guess how to use an attachment.

```text
Image 1 = layout reference only.
Image 2 = actual product image to place in the hero.
Attached ad = source of campaign angle.
Attached product = source of product / purchase information.
```

A screenshot can show a layout but not necessarily the intended interaction. If you want a selector, bundle, subscription choice, or other behavior to work a specific way, describe that separately.

## Select the area you want to change

For a page that is already close, edit in smaller passes.

**Better:**

```text
Change only the selected FAQ section.
Merge questions 2 and 4 because they answer the same objection.
Keep all approved answer facts, the section styling, and every other section unchanged.
```

**Less useful:**

```text
Make this page better.
```

Scoped edits reduce unintended changes and make it easier to review what happened.

## Protect working commerce behavior

When restyling a purchase area, say what must stay functional.

```text
Restyle the selected purchase section.
Keep the real product, variants, selling plans, quantities, prices,
and current Add to Cart / checkout behavior unchanged.
Do not turn functional selectors into static design cards.
Do not invent a discount or subscription plan.
```

A visual “Subscribe & Save” card does not create a real selling plan. A savings badge does not create a discount.

## Be specific about mobile

Instead of “make it responsive,” describe the hierarchy you want.

```text
Mobile only:
Stack copy above the image, keep the CTA full width,
and make sure the full product remains recognizable.
Keep desktop unchanged.
```

Then review both device modes after the AI edit.

## When the result is almost right

Do not repeat the whole original brief. Anchor what is correct and fix the remaining mismatch.

```text
The layout and copy are correct. Keep them.
Only fix the mobile image crop so the full bottle and cap remain visible.
Do not change desktop or any other section.
```

## Ask Jurni to review before editing

When you know something is wrong but do not yet know the right change:

```text
Do not edit yet.
Review this page for cold paid-social traffic.
Separate:
- visible page observations;
- hypotheses worth testing;
- things that require analytics, customer research, cart, or checkout verification.
Prioritize the top 5 issues.
```

This is safer than asking AI to make dozens of unsolicited CRO changes.

## AI Knowledge vs. this prompt

Use [AI Knowledge](/getting-started/setup-ai-knowledge) for context you want Jurni to reuse across many builds: brand, audience, positioning, product truths, approved claims, and voice.

Use the **current prompt** for this campaign's angle, offer, page structure, experiment, or temporary message.

A useful rule:

> **Evergreen truth goes in AI Knowledge. Campaign intent goes in the prompt.**

## AI still needs human QA

An AI response saying an edit is complete does not prove:

- the real cart item is correct;
- the discount works;
- the subscription plan is correct;
- a form reached Klaviyo / Postscript;
- a pixel fired in Meta / GA4;
- checkout works;
- a Smart Link routes as intended.

Review the page and test the real campaign journey before launch.
