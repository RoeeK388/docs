Jurni Buy Boxes are commerce sections that let shoppers select a product configuration and add it to cart directly from a Jurni experience. Use them when the page needs more than a simple CTA—for example, when shoppers need to choose a variant, quantity, bundle, or subscription option before purchasing.

## What a Buy Box controls

A Buy Box can bring the key purchase decisions into one section:

- Product and variant selection
- Product images, title, pricing, and compare-at pricing when available
- Quantity or purchase-option controls supported by the selected section
- One-time and subscription purchase options when selling plans are available
- Add-to-cart behavior
- Offer messaging and supporting content around the purchase decision

<Info>
A Buy Box controls the shopping interface on the Jurni page. Product inventory, variants, selling plans, and the final product price still originate from your connected commerce platform unless the section explicitly supports an offer configuration.
</Info>

## Add a Buy Box

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the experience">
    Open the Jurni experience you want to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a product section">
    Open the section library and choose the Buy Box or product purchase section that best matches the layout you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the product">
    Connect the section to the Shopify product you want the shopper to purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure purchase options">
    Choose the variants, selling plans, quantities, or bundle behavior that should be available in the section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Style and QA">
    Adjust the section styling, then preview the experience and test the full add-to-cart flow before publishing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Buy Box vs. product CTA

Use a **Buy Box** when the shopper needs to make a product decision on the page. Use a standard **CTA** when the button only needs to send the shopper somewhere else or trigger a simpler action.

| Use a Buy Box when | Use a CTA when |
| --- | --- |
| Shoppers choose variants | You are linking to another page |
| You offer one-time vs. subscription | No product selection is needed |
| You want add-to-cart on the page | The action is informational or navigational |
| You need bundle or quantity selection | Checkout happens elsewhere |

## Product data and availability

The options visible in a Buy Box depend on the product data available to Jurni. If a variant or selling plan is not available in Shopify or is not returned through the connected store configuration, it cannot be selected reliably in the Buy Box.

<Tip>
When a product setup changes in Shopify, re-open the Buy Box and verify the available variants and purchase options before sending traffic to the page.
</Tip>

## Test before publishing

Always test the full purchase path on desktop and mobile:

1. Select each important variant or purchase option.
2. Add the product to cart.
3. Confirm the expected product, variant, quantity, and selling plan are in cart.
4. Continue to checkout.
5. Confirm discounts or gifts configured in Shopify are applied as expected.
