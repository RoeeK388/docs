A **product** is what you sell. A **Buy Box** is the section on a page where shoppers choose their options and continue to cart or checkout.

Start by finding the right product, add a Buy Box to the page, then test the purchase. You do not need to configure every commerce feature to launch a simple product page.

## What belongs where?

| What you are changing | Where to work |
| --- | --- |
| The real product, variants, prices, availability, or subscription plans | Your connected commerce platform. |
| Shared product benefits, details, reviews, or variant media used by Jurni | **Products → Edit Product Data**. |
| The Buy Box layout, selected product, or button action on a page | The **page editor**. |
| Discounts, shipping, and the amount actually charged | Your connected cart and checkout setup. |

For example, editing the headline above a Buy Box does not change its selected product. Showing “20% off” in the design does not create a checkout discount.

## Set up your first Buy Box

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your product">
    Open **Products** in the correct shop. Check the product and variants you intend to sell. For a missing Shopify product, review the catalog filters and [product availability guide](/building-funnels/sync-new-products).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to the page">
    In the editor, insert a Buy Box from **Section Library** or use an approved Saved Section. Choose the product and purchasing action. Follow [Add a Buy Box](/products/add-buy-box).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Style and test it">
    Adjust the copy, media, and layout. Check both device views, make a purchase selection, and confirm the correct item and price in cart or checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add the options your campaign needs

Use [purchase options](/building-funnels/product-purchase-options) for variants and purchase choices, [subscriptions](/building-funnels/subscriptions-selling-plans) for recurring purchases, [bundles](/building-funnels/bundles) for multi-product offers, and [offers](/commerce/offers-overview) for promotions. Available controls depend on the Buy Box you use.

A simple button can be enough when your page only needs to send visitors to an existing product page. See [Buttons and actions](/build/cta-actions).

## Reuse without changing the wrong pages

A **Saved Section** gives you an independent copy. A **Global Section** keeps content shared. Product information edited in **Products** is also shared information, not a change limited to one page.

Before making a change for just one campaign, check [what your product edit affects](/products/editing-scope).

<Note>
  Shopify has a native Jurni connection. Headless and other platforms use the purchasing setup agreed for that store. Always test the final publishing destination; native Shopify Pages have their own [cart configuration](/shopify-pages/cart-config).
</Note>
