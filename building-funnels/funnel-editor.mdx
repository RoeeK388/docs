The Funnel Editor is the visual workspace where you customize every part of a funnel. It is fast, flexible, and requires no code. You can adjust layout, edit content, replace media, and apply styling in a live preview that closely reflects what your visitors will see.

## Accessing the Funnel Editor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Funnels">
    Go to the **Funnels** area in your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select or create a funnel">
    Open an existing funnel, or create a new one to start from scratch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the editor">
    You are taken directly into the Funnel Editor, ready to build.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Funnel Editor Layout

The editor is divided into three main areas. Each serves a different purpose and works together to give you full control over the funnel.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Canvas (Center)" icon="desktop">
    The Canvas shows a live preview of your funnel. You can view the full layout, select individual sections and elements, and see changes reflected in real time. The preview closely reflects the live experience your visitors will have.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Sections Panel (Left)" icon="layer-group">
    The left panel controls funnel structure. Add new sections, reorder existing ones, remove sections you no longer need, and select a section to edit it. Each section acts as a content block, such as a hero, product showcase, testimonials, or call to action.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Editing Panel (Right)" icon="sliders">
    The right panel changes based on what you select. Use it to edit text and copy, replace images and videos, adjust layout and spacing, configure products and CTAs, and modify styling and design details.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How Editing Works

Editing in the Funnel Editor is contextual. The right panel and available options change depending on what you click.

- **Click a section** to edit its structure and layout
- **Click text** to update copy and messaging
- **Click media** to replace images or videos
- **Click buttons or products** to configure behavior and links

<Note>
  Changes save automatically as you edit. There is no manual save button. Your work is preserved in the background while you build.
</Note>

## Funnel Status and Changes

All funnels start in **Draft** status. Edits you make while in Draft are not visible to visitors until you publish.

Once a funnel is published, you can continue editing. Updates remain private until you republish, so you can refine and preview changes before making them live.

## What the Funnel Editor Is Used For

You can use the Funnel Editor to accomplish a wide range of customization tasks:

- **Customize layout and structure**: Arrange sections and control the overall flow of the page.
- **Adjust messaging and visuals**: Update copy, headlines, images, and videos to match your offer.
- **Control product presentation**: Configure how products, pricing, and CTAs appear to visitors.
- **Apply brand styling**: Set colors, fonts, spacing, and design details that match your brand.
- **Prepare for publishing and traffic**: Finalize content and design before sending visitors to the funnel.

Each of these topics is covered in more detail in dedicated articles.
