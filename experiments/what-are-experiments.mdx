Use an **experiment** to compare page variants through one campaign link. For example, send some visitors to your current page and others to a version with a different hero, then compare purchase conversion and revenue.

The page is the destination, the Smart Link is the campaign entry URL, and the experiment manages the comparison.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the alternatives">
    Build and test the pages you want to compare. A variant can be a published Jurni page or a supported existing website URL. Custom destinations need the appropriate Jurni tracking connection for reporting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the test">
    Name the experiment, choose a new or available existing Smart Link, and set the variant destinations and traffic weights.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send traffic through the test link">
    Put the connected Smart Link in your campaign. Going directly to a variant page does not automatically enter the test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare results and decide">
    Review the variants using the same date range and the metric chosen for your hypothesis. When ending a test, deliberately choose the destination that should continue receiving traffic.
  </Step>
</Steps>

[Create an experiment](/experiments/create-experiment) provides the setup steps.

## What to understand before launching

**Weights describe the intended split, not an exact visitor-by-visitor quota.** Small samples can be uneven, and returning visitors can retain an assignment. Refreshing one browser repeatedly does not test the allocation.

**Routing Rules are optional.** They can direct matching URL-parameter traffic to a specific variant before the normal split. That is useful for campaign routing, but is a different traffic setup from a simple randomized comparison.

**Reporting depends on the measurement setup.** A link can open an external page successfully without its required events being tracked. Check the purchase path and measurement before launching.

## Choose a useful comparison

Use a clear hypothesis and a primary metric. CVR measures orders relative to sessions; AOV measures revenue per order; RPS measures revenue per session. Interaction rates can help explain where shoppers progress or drop off.

Do not choose a winner simply because one number is temporarily higher. See [Reading results](/experiments/reading-results) and [Testing best practices](/experiments/strategic-experiments). Exact definitions belong in the [metrics glossary](/data-dictionary/metrics-glossary).

## Keep the campaign link stable

When your campaign already uses the experiment's Smart Link, you can manage the test behind that entry URL. Page changes, variant changes, pausing, and ending are still different actions with different effects.

<Warning>
  Before pausing or ending a test with live traffic, check where visitors will go. The current pause dialog warns that traffic will go to the shop home page; ending asks you to select the winning destination. Read [Pause, end, and reuse](/experiments/pause-end-reuse) first.
</Warning>
