Jurni Experiments let you route traffic between two or more destinations and compare their performance. Experiments can use a Smart Link as the stable campaign URL, so you can change the destinations or traffic split in Jurni without editing the URL already placed in your ads.

## How Experiments Work

When a shopper clicks your SmartLink, Jurni routes them to one of your variants based on your traffic allocation. Every session is tracked and attributed to the correct variant automatically.

Key details:

- **Stable campaign URL:** When the experiment uses a Smart Link, the URL placed in your campaign can remain unchanged while Jurni controls the destinations behind it.
- **Traffic weights:** Each variant can receive a configured share of traffic. Small samples will not always match the configured split exactly.
- **Routing rules:** You can route visitors to specific variants based on URL parameters before the normal weighted split is applied.
- **Current performance metrics:** Jurni experiment reporting includes sessions, orders, revenue, CTR, CVR, add-to-cart rate, checkout rate, AOV, and revenue per session (RPS).
- **What you can test:** Experiments can compare Jurni experiences and supported external destinations, such as an existing Shopify page.

<Tip>
  Experiments help you compare page variants while keeping the campaign-facing Smart Link stable. Use them when you need evidence before deciding which page to scale.
</Tip>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to change the URL in my ads to update an experiment?">
    Not when the ads already use the Smart Link for that traffic. You can change the destination or use the Smart Link with an experiment from Jurni while keeping the campaign-facing URL stable.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What metrics does Jurni track?">
    Jurni tracks CVR, AOV, RPS, add-to-cart rate, and checkout rate for each variant. All metrics update in near-real-time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I test against my existing Shopify product page?">
    Yes. You can add an external URL as a variant. See [Testing Against Your Website](/experiments/testing-against-website) for details on how attribution works.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
