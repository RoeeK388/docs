Jurni Experiments are the built-in A/B testing framework that lets you split traffic between two or more funnels to find what converts better. Unlike running separate campaigns, experiments use a single SmartLink to split traffic, so your Meta or Google ad set stays untouched and you never push ads back into the learning phase.

## How Experiments Work

When a shopper clicks your SmartLink, Jurni routes them to one of your variants based on your traffic allocation. Every session is tracked and attributed to the correct variant automatically.

Key details:

- **No learning phase reset.** Your ad set destination URL never changes, so Meta and Google algorithms are not disrupted.
- **Metrics tracked:** CVR, AOV, RPS, add-to-cart rate, checkout rate.
- **Results update in near-real-time** as sessions and purchases come in.
- **What you can test:** Jurni funnel vs. Jurni funnel, or a Jurni funnel vs. your existing Shopify product page or any external URL.
- **Requirement:** Experiments require a SmartLink. You must have at least two published funnels, or one published funnel and one external URL, to create an experiment.

<Tip>
  Experiments are the fastest way to find your highest-converting funnel without touching your ad campaigns. Set one up before scaling spend.
</Tip>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to pause my ads to run an experiment?">
    No. Experiments route traffic through your SmartLink. Your ad campaign destination URL stays the same, so there is no disruption to ad delivery or algorithm optimization.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What metrics does Jurni track?">
    Jurni tracks CVR, AOV, RPS, add-to-cart rate, and checkout rate for each variant. All metrics update in near-real-time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I test against my existing Shopify product page?">
    Yes. You can add an external URL as a variant. See [Testing Against Your Website](/experiments/testing-against-website) for details on how attribution works.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a paid plan?">
    Experiments are available on all Jurni plans.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
