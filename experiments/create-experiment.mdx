Jurni experiments let you test agentic shopping journeys against each other using a single SmartLink. You drop one URL into your ads, Jurni splits traffic across your variants, and when a winner is clear you can route all future traffic to it without changing your campaign links. This page walks you through creating an experiment from setup to finish.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your experiment">
    Open the **Experiments** tab and select **Create Experiment** in the top right corner.

    Name your experiment with two labels:

    - **Test Name**: your internal name for finding the experiment inside Jurni.
    - **Link Name**: the external name used in the SmartLink itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your variants">
    Select **Add Variant** for each journey you want to test. You can add up to 10 variants.

    For every variant, choose one of two sources:

    - An agentic shopping journey already built in Jurni.
    - A custom URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the traffic split">
    Jurni distributes traffic evenly across your variants by default. A two-variant experiment splits 50/50, a four-variant experiment splits 25% each, and so on.

    To use a different distribution, adjust the weight on each variant to set how much traffic it receives. This is useful when you want to favor a proven journey while still testing a new one, or to limit exposure on an experimental variant until the early data looks right.

    <Tip>
      Because every variant runs on the same SmartLink, traffic routes automatically based on the weights you set the moment your experiment goes live.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and start the test">
    When you are ready, select **Save and Start Test**. Your experiment goes live immediately and you land on the experiment dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage a live experiment

From the dashboard you can:

- Review your metrics, with an overview and a breakdown by variant.
- Edit existing variants, or add a new one at any time.
- See a full history of every change made.
- Select **Copy Link** to bring the SmartLink URL straight into your ads.
- Pause the experiment whenever you need to hold traffic.

## End an experiment and name a winner

When one variant clearly wins, follow these steps to close the test and route all traffic to the winner:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select End">
    On the experiment dashboard, select **End** to begin closing the experiment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the winning variant">
    Pick the variant that performed best based on your metrics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm to end the experiment">
    Confirm your choice. Because the link is a SmartLink, all traffic from that point forward routes automatically to your winning variant. No new URL to swap into your ads, no campaign downtime.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Ending an experiment is final. Once you confirm the winner, traffic will no longer split and all visitors will be routed to the selected variant.
</Warning>
