An experiment sends visitors from one campaign link to different page variants so you can compare their results. Prepare the pages first, then configure the test and use its connected Smart Link in your campaign.

## Before you start

Decide what you are testing and which metric will guide your decision. For example: “Does a shorter hero improve purchase conversion?” Start with your existing page and one meaningful alternative rather than changing every part of the journey at once.

Test both pages on mobile and desktop, including their product options and checkout. A custom URL also needs the appropriate Jurni tracking connection to report results; successfully redirecting traffic there is not enough.

To prepare an alternative with AI, duplicate the experience and ask for the specific change in the copy. For example: “Change only the hero headline to the approved alternative below. Keep the offer, products, button actions, and other sections unchanged.” Review and publish the alternative before selecting it as a variant. See [Prompt examples](/build/prompt-library) and [Manage experiences](/build/manage-experiences).

## Set up the experiment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the test">
    Open the tests/experiments area and start a new experiment. Enter a **Test Name** you will recognize later. Use the optional **Description** to record the hypothesis.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the campaign link">
    Under **Link**, choose **Create new link** and enter its slug, or **Use existing link** and select an available Smart Link. The existing-link picker excludes links already connected to experiments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the page variants">
    For each variant, set its **Variant Name** and **Destination**. Select a published Jurni page or enter the supported custom URL. Use names that describe the difference, such as “Current hero” and “Short hero.”
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Weight %">
    The initial two variants start at 50% each. Adjust their weights if needed. Adding or removing a variant redistributes the weights, so review the final split before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add routing rules only when needed">
    Use [Routing Rules](/experiments/routing-rules) when specific campaign parameters must reach a particular variant. This is separate from a simple weighted comparison; leave rules out when you do not need that routing behavior.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and start">
    Review the destinations and link, then use the form's save/start action. Confirm any warning about replacing an existing link's destination. Wait for the success message and open the experiment overview.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Reusing a Smart Link that is already in ads or emails can change where that existing traffic goes when you start the test. Do not treat this as a private draft operation.
</Warning>

## Launch through the right URL

Copy the experiment's connected Smart Link into the campaign. Open that exact URL with the campaign parameters and check the result. Direct visits to a variant page do not automatically belong to the experiment.

A returning visitor can keep their assignment. Repeatedly refreshing one browser is not a meaningful check of whether a 50/50 split is working.

Use [Test your campaign link](/publishing/check-campaign-link) before sending traffic.

## Review or change a running test

Read the variant metrics using the same date range and your chosen decision metric. Check [Reading results](/experiments/reading-results) before interpreting a small difference as a winner.

The variant-editing form locks the destination URL of an existing variant. Do not assume you can replace its URL in place. Record any changes to the test or underlying page, since combining results from different page versions can make the comparison harder to interpret.

For traffic weights and returning visitors, see [Traffic assignment](/experiments/traffic-assignment).

## Pause or finish deliberately

Before pausing, read the confirmation: the current pause flow warns that visitors will be sent to the shop home page. Ending is a different action that asks you to choose the winning destination.

Follow [Pause, end, and reuse](/experiments/pause-end-reuse) before changing a test with live campaign traffic. Selecting a winner is a routing decision; it is not, by itself, proof of statistical significance.
