Use **Offers** to configure promotions for eligible Jurni experiences connected to **Shopify**. Offers are separate from the Buy Box design and from the general controls in Storefront Settings.

<Note>
  Non-Shopify and custom cart flows need an agreed implementation with the Jurni team. These instructions do not automatically configure WooCommerce, Salesforce, a headless cart, or an external checkout.
</Note>

## Choose the offer type

| Offer | What you configure in Jurni | What must also be correct in Shopify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Gift with Purchase](/building-funnels/gift-with-purchase) | The gift products, qualifying cart conditions, and gift selection behavior. | Gift availability and the rule or price that makes the gift free. |
| [Free Shipping](/commerce/free-shipping-offers) | A qualifying amount or quantity, with an optional subscription qualification. | The shipping rate or shipping discount available at checkout. |
| [Samples](/commerce/samples-offers) | Eligible sample products, the selection limit, and the minimum qualifying amount. | Sample prices and any applicable discount. Samples are not automatically free. |
| [Discount Code](/commerce/discount-code-offers) | An existing Shopify code, where it applies in Jurni, and optional promotional display. | The code's value, eligibility, combinations, purchase types, dates, and usage limits. |

The current offer picker does not expose separate **Order Discount**, **Buy More Save More**, or **Product Discount** creation flows. A custom bundle or Buy Box can still need a promotion, but its discount must be implemented separately.

## Create an offer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Offers and choose a type">
    Create a new offer and select one of the available types above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the offer">
    Use an internal name your team can recognize. **Display Text**, where provided, is the separate message shown to shoppers by supported components.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Offer Scope">
    **Global** makes the offer available across eligible experiences in this shop. To limit it, use the selected-experiences option and choose the intended pages. This control may still display the legacy label **Funnels**. It is not a rule targeting every page on your entire Shopify website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set timing and rules">
    Add start/end timing when needed, then configure the conditions for the selected offer type. Check the displayed currency for amount thresholds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Save the offer, check its status in Offers, and test an included experience. Confirm both the shopper-facing message and the actual checkout result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Jurni messaging is not discount enforcement

**Creating an offer does not mean Jurni created its discount in Shopify.** The current **Create Shopify Discount** control shown on some offer forms is disabled. Configure the actual discount or shipping rule in Shopify or your discount app.

For example, “Spend $$75, get a free gift” needs both a qualifying Jurni gift rule and a Shopify setup that charges $$0 for the intended gift. A correct banner with a full-priced gift is not a completed setup.

## Check eligibility before launch

Test below and above the threshold, the intended one-time/subscription purchase, and both an included and excluded experience. Remove a qualifying item and check what happens. For multiple currencies, repeat the test in the relevant market.

For Shopify Pages or a third-party cart, confirm that the selected offer has been connected to that cart. Do not assume Jurni cart banners and gift-selection behavior are installed in the theme cart automatically.
