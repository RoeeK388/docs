Use **Offers** when you want a Shopify-connected Jurni campaign to present and enforce one of the supported promotional rules.

The easiest way to start is with the campaign you want to run—not with the name of a Jurni setting.

## What promotion are you trying to run?

If your campaign needs a different discount mechanic—such as custom tiered pricing, a bundle discount, or an offer controlled by another Shopify app—the pricing logic may live outside Jurni's current Offers area. Confirm the implementation rather than trying to recreate it as display copy.

## The rule to remember

**Jurni controls the supported campaign / cart experience. Shopify controls the actual commercial result at checkout.**

For example, a campaign that says:

> Spend \$75 and get a free travel-size product

needs both:

1. a Jurni Gift with Purchase rule that knows when the cart qualifies and which gift is eligible, and
2. a Shopify price / discount setup that makes that gift actually free.

A correct-looking offer banner with a full-priced gift in checkout is not a completed setup.

## The basic Shopify offer workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the commercial rule first">
    Write the real qualification, product(s), purchase type, discount, and timing. Make sure the same approved rule is understood by whoever manages Shopify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the corresponding Jurni offer">
    Open **Offers**, choose Gift with Purchase, Free Shipping, Samples, or Discount Code, and enter the settings for that offer type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where it applies">
    Use **Global** when the offer should apply across eligible Jurni experiences in the shop, or choose the specific experiences for the campaign. The current UI may still use the legacy word **Funnels** in this scope control.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add approved timing and display copy">
    Set dates when needed and use shopper-facing copy that accurately describes the real rule. The offer's internal name is for your team; Display Text, where available, is customer-facing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Shopify enforcement">
    Check the discount, product price, or shipping setup that produces the promised checkout result. The current **Create Shopify Discount** control shown in some Jurni offer forms is disabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the offer from page to checkout">
    Test a qualifying purchase and one that should not qualify. Check the actual products, price, gift, shipping, and purchase type—not only the page message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Which system should I edit?

| You want to change… | Usually edit… |
| --- | --- |
| Which Jurni experiences the promotion applies to | Jurni Offers |
| The GWP qualification or gift-selection behavior supported by Jurni | Jurni Offers |
| Which samples appear and when they unlock | Jurni Offers |
| The promotional message shown by supported Jurni components | Jurni Offers / page content |
| The actual Shopify discount value | Shopify / your discount app |
| Whether a gift is charged \$0 | Shopify / your discount setup |
| Shopify shipping rates / actual free-shipping eligibility | Shopify |
| A discount code's usage limits, customer eligibility, combinations, or purchase restrictions | Shopify |
| General Jurni cart display options | [Storefront Settings → Cart Settings](/commerce/cart-display-settings) |

## Test the edge cases that can break a promotion

Before launch, check what happens when the shopper:

- stays just below the threshold
- crosses the threshold
- removes a qualifying item
- switches from subscription to one-time or vice versa when relevant
- changes product quantities
- uses another discount code that may combine or conflict
- visits from a different Shopify market / currency when relevant

The right test depends on your offer. You do not need every scenario for every campaign.

## Shopify Pages and third-party carts

A Jurni offer configured for the supported Jurni cart does not automatically install its banner, gift selector, or other cart UI into a Shopify theme drawer or a third-party cart such as Rebuy.

If the campaign publishes as a native Shopify Page, confirm the theme/cart connection separately. See [Shopify Pages cart connection](/shopify-pages/cart-config).

For headless and non-Shopify stores, use the purchase / offer implementation agreed with the Jurni team rather than these Shopify-specific steps.
