A Samples offer lets shoppers choose from a configured list of sample products after their cart reaches a minimum order amount.

## Current configuration

The current Samples offer supports:

- selecting one or more **sample products**
- setting how many samples the shopper can choose
- setting a **minimum order amount** required to unlock samples

The cart threshold is calculated against the order amount used for qualification, excluding the samples themselves.

## Sample product price

The Samples editor allows you to select products regardless of their catalog price. Selecting a product as a sample does not by itself change the product's Shopify price.

Make sure your Shopify/commerce configuration produces the intended final price for the selected sample products.

## Shopper flow

Once the cart reaches the configured minimum amount, the shopper can select up to the maximum number of samples configured for the offer.

If the cart falls back below the eligibility threshold, test how your specific cart/promotion configuration handles already-selected samples before launch.

## QA checklist

Verify:

1. the sample selector is hidden before qualification
2. it appears at the configured order threshold
3. the correct products are available
4. the shopper cannot select more than the configured maximum
5. selected samples reach the cart correctly
6. sample pricing is correct in Shopify checkout
7. dropping below the threshold does not leave an invalid cart state
