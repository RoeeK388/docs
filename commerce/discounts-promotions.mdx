Jurni can present an offer and add the intended products to cart, but the discount itself must be enforced by Shopify or the discount system used by your store.

## Separate the page experience from discount enforcement

A promotion has two layers:

1. **The Jurni experience** explains the offer and controls the shopper's product selection.
2. **Shopify or your discount app** determines the final price in cart and checkout.

<Warning>
Never publish savings messaging until you have confirmed the same promotion is applied in the actual cart and checkout.
</Warning>

## Common discount setups

Your store may use:

- Shopify automatic discounts
- Discount codes
- Shopify Functions
- Third-party discount applications
- Product pricing or compare-at pricing
- Dedicated promotional products or variants

Jurni does not require every offer to use the same discount method. Use the approach that matches your Shopify architecture.

## Automatic discounts

Automatic discounts are useful when the shopper should receive an offer without entering a code. Make sure the qualifying products, quantities, and thresholds match the product configuration on the Jurni page.

## Discount codes

If your campaign relies on a code, decide whether the shopper must enter it or whether your commerce setup applies it automatically. Test the code with the exact product and variant combinations used on the page.

## Threshold and quantity promotions

For offers such as "buy 3, save 20%" or "spend $100, get a free gift," test boundary cases:

- One unit below the required quantity
- Exactly at the required quantity
- Above the required quantity
- Just below and above spend thresholds
- Combination with other discounts

## Gift with purchase

For a GWP offer, Jurni can add the gift product while Shopify controls how that product is discounted. See the dedicated Gift With Purchase guide for the full setup model.

## QA checklist

Before launch, verify:

- The page displays the correct offer.
- The qualifying products are correct.
- The cart receives the expected line items.
- The discount is applied at the expected point.
- The shopper sees the same final price in checkout.
- Discount stacking behaves as intended.
