A Free Shipping offer lets Jurni show and evaluate free-shipping eligibility in the storefront cart.

The current offer editor supports two trigger types:

- **Minimum Amount** — unlock free shipping when the cart reaches a configured value.
- **Minimum Product Quantity** — unlock free shipping when the cart contains at least a configured number of items.

## Subscription option

For either trigger type, you can enable **Offer free shipping with subscription products**.

When enabled, a cart that contains a subscription product can qualify for the free-shipping offer regardless of the normal cart threshold, and the cart messaging can present both paths to eligibility.

## Shopify discount enforcement

The Jurni offer controls the storefront/cart eligibility and messaging. The final shipping discount must still be valid in Shopify checkout.

Jurni's current **Create Shopify Discount** control in the offer editor is not an editable self-service control. Do not rely on creating the Jurni offer alone to create a new Shopify free-shipping discount.

<Warning>
Configure and verify the actual free-shipping discount in Shopify or your store's discount logic. Test checkout—not only the Jurni cart banner.
</Warning>

## QA checklist

Test:

1. below the threshold
2. exactly at the threshold
3. above the threshold
4. adding/removing items after qualification
5. subscription and one-time carts if the subscription option is enabled
6. the final shipping charge in Shopify checkout
