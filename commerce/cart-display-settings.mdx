Use **Settings → Storefront Settings → Cart Settings** for small changes to the supported Jurni cart on Shopify-connected experiences.

<Note>
  This is not a cart builder. These settings do not redesign Shopify checkout, a Shopify theme cart, or a third-party cart such as Rebuy. For other commerce platforms, contact the Jurni team about your [purchase integration](/commerce/non-shopify-purchase-flows).
</Note>

## Available controls

| Control | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Cart title** | The heading at the top of the cart. |
| **Remove decimals from whole prices** | Shows a whole price such as $$20 instead of $$20.00. It does not change the price charged. |
| **Show discount code field** | Shows or hides the field where shoppers enter a code. Hiding the field does not disable every discount already applied by Shopify. |
| **Show shipping calculator** | Shows or hides the cart's shipping estimate control. It does not create or change your shipping rates. |
| **Show prices on buttons** | Shows the relevant price on supported cart action buttons. |

## Change and save

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the correct shop">
    Check the shop name, then open **Settings → Storefront Settings** and find **Cart Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the controls">
    Enter the cart title or change the relevant switches. These settings belong to the shop, not only the page you last edited.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Select **Save Changes** in the Cart Settings area and wait for confirmation. **Reset** returns unsaved form changes to their saved values; it does not empty a shopper's cart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the cart">
    Open a published Jurni-hosted page, add a real product, and verify the changed display on desktop and mobile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What needs a different setup?

Use **Offers** for supported promotional rules, not Cart Settings. Configure actual discount and shipping rules in Shopify. For a custom cart layout, additional upsells, subscription-frequency behavior, or another cart provider, discuss the requirement with the Jurni team rather than looking for controls that are not in this panel.

When using **Shopify Pages**, follow [Shopify Pages cart configuration](/shopify-pages/cart-config). Settings for Jurni's cart do not automatically control the theme's drawer.
