The cart / checkout experience depends on **where your page is running** and **which commerce setup your store already uses**.

Jurni does not replace every Shopify theme cart or custom storefront cart with one universal cart. Start with the shopper outcome you need.

## What happens when someone clicks a purchase button?

### Add to cart

Adds the selected real product choice to the connected cart.

Depending on the purchase section, that can include:

- product / variant;
- quantity;
- subscription selling plan when selected;
- other supported product data.

The next visual experience depends on the publishing / cart environment.

### Go to checkout

Takes the configured purchase selection into checkout instead of asking the shopper to review a cart first.

### External URL

Sends the shopper to the URL you configured—for example, an existing store PDP or another purchase flow.

It is a navigation action. It does **not** add the visible product to cart just because the button text says “Buy now.”

[Buttons & actions →](/build/cta-actions)

## If my page is Jurni-hosted on Shopify, which cart does it use?

Jurni-hosted Shopify experiences can use the supported Jurni storefront/cart flow connected to the Shopify shop.

Your shop's **Storefront Settings → Cart Settings** provides only a small set of general display controls:

- cart title;
- remove decimals from whole prices;
- show / hide discount-code field;
- show / hide shipping calculator;
- show prices on cart buttons.

This is **not a drag-and-drop cart builder**.

[See the exact cart settings →](/commerce/cart-display-settings)

Shopify / the store's commerce apps still determine things such as:

- inventory;
- real product prices;
- discount validity;
- shipping rates;
- tax;
- subscription contracts;
- final checkout.

## Can I use my existing Shopify theme cart or Rebuy?

If the page is published as a **native Shopify Page**, it runs inside your Shopify theme environment.

That means the store may already use:

- the theme's native cart drawer;
- Rebuy;
- another cart app;
- custom theme JavaScript.

Jurni can be connected to that existing cart behavior where supported, but there is no universal “use Rebuy” switch that configures every theme / version automatically.

Use [Shopify Pages cart connection](/shopify-pages/cart-config) and test the actual `/pages/` destination.

If your theme / cart app needs custom post-add-to-cart behavior, your developer or the Jurni team should confirm the correct integration rather than pasting a random snippet from another store.

## Can I customize the cart in Jurni?

For the supported Jurni Shopify cart, the self-service settings today are the general display controls listed above.

You **cannot currently use Storefront Settings as a full cart designer** to:

- rearrange arbitrary cart sections;
- build custom upsell / cross-sell journeys;
- design every cart component;
- replace Shopify checkout;
- redefine subscription behavior;
- create a custom discount engine.

If your campaign needs a more custom cart experience, discuss the desired shopper journey with the Jurni team before assuming there is a hidden cart-editor control.

## What if I am not on Shopify?

Do not follow the Shopify cart instructions for WooCommerce, Salesforce, a custom commerce stack, or another headless frontend.

Jurni can work with the purchase flow your business already uses. Common patterns include:

### Existing cart API

The Jurni page sends the selected product / purchase data into your existing cart integration.

### Direct checkout link

The CTA sends the shopper to a checkout URL created by your commerce system.

### Existing product page

The landing page does the education / campaign storytelling, then sends the customer to your existing PDP to choose / purchase.

### Custom solution

A quiz, bundle, subscription, or other unique flow may require a custom implementation with your team and Jurni.

[Headless & non-Shopify purchase paths →](/commerce/non-shopify-purchase-flows)

Shopify Headless is also a separate case: Shopify can still be the commerce backend without the frontend using a Shopify theme drawer.

## What about offers and discounts?

The page can explain an offer, but the real cart / checkout still needs the actual commercial rule.

Examples:

- free gift → actual Shopify gift pricing / discount;
- free shipping → real eligible shipping rule;
- discount code → real Shopify code;
- subscription savings → real selling plan / price;
- bundle savings → real configured bundle / discount behavior.

Use [Offers](/commerce/offers-overview) for the supported Shopify campaign rules.

## How do I test the purchase flow?

Start from the **actual campaign URL**, not just Preview.

For an important campaign, test:

1. default product choice;
2. key variants;
3. one-time / subscription when relevant;
4. quantity;
5. Add to Cart / Go to Checkout behavior;
6. real cart item and plan;
7. discount / gift / shipping result;
8. checkout;
9. mobile behavior;
10. back-navigation if it matters to the campaign.

The page is not ready because the button looks correct. It is ready when the customer reaches the intended cart / checkout with the exact product and commercial promise you advertised.

## Common questions

### The button says Add to Cart but no drawer opens

The product may have been added even if the intended theme / third-party cart drawer was not opened. Check the cart contents and the publishing environment, then follow the relevant Shopify Pages cart setup.

### Can Jurni clear the cart every time someone returns?

Do not assume browser back-navigation clears the existing cart or creates a fresh checkout. If your campaign depends on a specific fresh-cart behavior, raise that requirement with the Jurni team and test it explicitly.

### Does changing button text change the action?

No. The displayed label and the configured button / purchase action are separate. A button can say “Buy now” while still being configured to Add to Cart.

### Does hiding the discount-code field disable discounts?

No. The cart display control hides that input. It does not disable Shopify automatic discounts or other discount logic.
