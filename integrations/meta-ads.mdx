## Meta Ad Accounts vs. Meta Pixel

Jurni has two different Meta integrations:

- **Meta Ad Accounts** connects Jurni to Meta's campaign/ad-account data through an authorization flow.
- **Meta Pixel** is a browser-side tracking integration configured with one or more Pixel IDs under Data & Tracking.

Connecting one does not automatically configure the other.

## Connect Meta Ad Accounts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Integrations">
    Find **Meta Ad Accounts** under Ad Integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Meta">
    Use the Meta connect action and complete Meta's authorization flow with a user who has access to the intended ad accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the ad accounts Jurni should use">
    After the Meta account is connected, open **Manage Accounts**. Jurni can work with all accessible accounts or a selected subset.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the connection">
    The integration shows **Active** when Meta is connected. Use **View Ad Accounts** to inspect the accounts available to Jurni.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Account selection

If no subset is selected, Jurni treats all accessible Meta ad accounts as selected. If you only want Jurni to use certain ad accounts, choose them in the account manager.

This selection affects the Meta account/campaign data Jurni can surface for features such as Strategy/Opportunities and Meta performance analysis.

## If Jurni shows “No Accounts Found”

The Meta authorization succeeded, but the authenticated Meta user does not currently expose any accessible ad accounts to Jurni.

Check:

- the Meta user has access to the correct Business/Ad Account
- the expected ad account is active
- the authorization was completed with the intended Meta login

You can reconnect Meta after correcting the Meta-side access.

## Remove Meta

Use **Remove Meta Integration** from the integration's actions menu to disconnect the Meta account connection.

Removing Meta Ad Accounts does not by itself remove a Meta Pixel ID saved under Data & Tracking. They are separate integrations.
