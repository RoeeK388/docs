## The integration workflow

Most integrations have two or three steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the provider">
    Open **Settings → Integrations**, choose the correct brand account, and enter or authorize the value Jurni asks for. The provider-specific guide tells you exactly where to find it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure where it should appear or what it should track">
    A connected review provider still needs a review widget on the page. A connected Klaviyo account still needs the intended form or pop-up setup. A GTM container still needs its actual tags and triggers configured in GTM.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the real published page">
    Open the final campaign destination and perform the action you care about. Verify the review, signup, or event in the third-party platform—not only the “connected” state in Jurni.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review providers

Choose the platform your brand already uses:

[Okendo](/integrations/okendo) · [Yotpo](/integrations/yotpo) · [Junip](/integrations/junip) · [Judge.me](/integrations/judge-me) · [Stamped](/integrations/stamped) · [Trustpilot](/integrations/trustpilot) · [REVIEWS.io](/integrations/reviews-io) · [Bazaarvoice](/integrations/bazaarvoice)

The workflow is usually:

**connect provider → add its review section → choose the correct product / widget / SKU when required → publish → compare the reviews with the provider.**

Do not paste screenshots of reviews when the goal is to keep the page connected to the live review provider.

## Forms and pop-ups

Use [Klaviyo](/integrations/klaviyo) for supported embedded forms and Klaviyo pop-up control. Use [Postscript](/integrations/postscript) for supported SMS signup forms and pop-ups. Use [Alia](/integrations/alia) for its supported pop-up script connection.

A shop-level connection and a page-level form are different things. If the account is connected but the page still shows a placeholder, complete the form/widget setup on the page.

## Meta Ads is not Meta Pixel

These are intentionally separate:

- **Meta Ads connection** → lets Jurni work with the selected advertising account and related ad data.
- **Meta Pixel** → loads the configured website pixel for supported browser tracking.

Many brands use both. Connecting one does not automatically configure the other.

## Shopify Pages need a second check

A native Shopify Page runs inside the Shopify theme, where Klaviyo, review apps, GTM, pixels, and cart apps may already load.

Before adding another installation, test the actual `/pages/` destination and check what the theme already provides. Duplicate installations can create duplicate events or widgets.

## Headless and non-Shopify stores

A provider connection does not automatically solve product mapping, cart/checkout attribution, or a custom storefront's script environment. For those setups, use the provider guide for the Jurni side and confirm the storefront implementation with the Jurni team.
