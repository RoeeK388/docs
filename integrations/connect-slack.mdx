Connect Jurni to Slack when you want a Slack workspace to work with your Jurni shop and receive scheduled performance reports.

## Connection and reporting are separate

Connecting Slack does **not** automatically start sending performance reports.

There are two separate steps:

1. Connect the Jurni shop to a Slack workspace from **Settings → Integrations**.
2. Configure Daily or Weekly reports, channels, and schedules from the Slack notification settings.

## Who can manage Slack

Slack management is restricted to users with the required Jurni permissions. If the install or disconnect controls are disabled, ask a Jurni owner/admin or a teammate with messaging-management access to complete the change.

Your Slack workspace may also require an administrator to approve app installation.

## Connect a workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Integrations">
    Find **Slack** in the Messaging section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Add to Slack">
    If a workspace is already connected, the button changes to **Add another workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the Slack workspace">
    Jurni redirects you into Slack's install/authorization flow. Choose the intended workspace and approve the requested permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Jurni">
    The connected workspace now appears under the Slack integration for the current Jurni shop.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Multiple Slack workspaces

A Jurni shop can be connected to more than one Slack workspace. Each connected workspace is listed separately in **Settings → Integrations** and can have its own notification configuration.

## Shop access inside Slack

Connecting a shop to a Slack workspace makes that shop available to the Jurni Slack app, but it does **not** automatically give every person in the workspace permission to all shop data.

Individual users connect/authenticate their own Jurni access when required. Shop access is managed from the Jurni App Home in Slack, and Jurni still enforces the user's underlying product access.

## Disconnect a workspace

In **Settings → Integrations**, open the menu beside a connected Slack workspace and choose **Disconnect**.

Disconnecting removes that shop from the workspace. People in that Slack workspace will no longer be able to work with the shop's data through Jurni.

<Info>
  If you only want to stop automated performance reports, do not disconnect Slack. Disable or edit the report subscription instead.
</Info>

## Configure performance reports

After Slack is connected, configure reporting for each workspace:

- choose a Slack channel
- enable **Daily report** and/or **Weekly report**
- set the send time and timezone
- choose the weekday for weekly reports
- send a test message before relying on the schedule

## Slack Connect / external channels

Jurni detects Slack Connect channels that may include people outside your workspace. Before sending shop metrics to an external channel, Jurni requires an explicit acknowledgement.

<Warning>
  Only send performance reports to a Slack Connect channel when everyone with access to that channel is allowed to see the shop's performance data.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot install or disconnect Slack">
    Confirm your Jurni role/permissions allow Slack management and that Slack permits you to install apps in the target workspace.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Slack is connected but no reports are being sent">
    Connection alone does not create a report subscription. Open the Slack notification settings and confirm a channel and Daily/Weekly schedule are enabled.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I need to connect a second workspace">
    Open the Slack integration and select **Add another workspace**. Existing workspace connections remain intact.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
