Jurni Playbooks are repeatable workflows for the marketing jobs your team does again and again. They are not rigid templates and they are not feature documentation.

Each playbook gives you the strategy, sequence, prompts, review criteria, and next learning step for a specific outcome.

## What makes something a playbook?

A playbook should be reusable across brands without becoming generic. That means it captures **the decision process**, not a fixed page design.

For example, the Advertorial playbook does not say every page must have exactly eight sections. It teaches the job each part of the story needs to do, how to brief Jurni, and how to know when the page has become too sales-heavy or too editorial to convert.

Likewise, the Experiment playbook does not prescribe a universal amount of traffic or a guaranteed winning metric. It teaches how to isolate a hypothesis, route the correct traffic, verify the experience, and interpret the observed result responsibly.
