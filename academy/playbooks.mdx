## How to use a playbook

Start with the workflow that matches the **traffic and customer decision**, not the page style you happen to prefer. Bring your real campaign inputs: ads, audience, product, offer, approved proof, and any design reference.

Every playbook includes a copy-ready Jurni prompt, but the prompt is only one part of the workflow. Review the result, verify real product / purchase behavior, and test the final campaign URL before sending traffic.

For exact product controls, use the Help Center. For deep AI briefing techniques, start with [Prompting 101](/academy/prompting-101).
