These prompts help Jurni present an approved campaign or offer clearly. They do **not** create a discount, selling plan, shipping rule, or gift logic by themselves. Configure the real commerce behavior in the appropriate Jurni / Shopify flow and verify checkout.

## Match a Meta ad to the landing page

```text
Use the attached Meta ad as the primary campaign reference.

Audience: [AUDIENCE].
Ad angle: [ANGLE].
Approved offer: [OFFER].
Primary action: [ACTION].

Update the page so the first screen continues the same story:
- headline should reflect the ad's core promise;
- use the same product emphasis;
- keep the offer consistent;
- keep the CTA expectation consistent;
- carry the visual tone without copying the ad as static artwork.

Below the fold, add only the education and proof needed to help this audience make the purchase decision.
Do not invent claims, reviews, discounts, or urgency.
```

## Match a TikTok / UGC ad

```text
Build / update this landing page for traffic from the attached TikTok / UGC creative.

The creative works because it feels [CASUAL / DEMONSTRATIVE / ROUTINE-BASED / PROBLEM-FIRST].
Keep that same customer language and use case on the page without turning the page into a fake social post.

Use:
- creator asset / quote only where supplied;
- connected product for real purchase information;
- AI Knowledge for approved claims.

Prioritize mobile, fast scanning, and product recognition.
Do not invent creator results, endorsement language, or discount codes.
```

## Seasonal sale page

```text
Create a [BLACK FRIDAY / HOLIDAY / SEASONAL] campaign page.
Audience: [AUDIENCE].
Traffic source: [CHANNEL].
Approved offer: [EXACT OFFER].
Start / end timing if approved: [TIMING].

The page should make the offer easy to understand without overwhelming the product story.

Include:
1. Offer-led hero
2. Clear explanation of what is included / excluded
3. Featured products or approved purchase options
4. Trust / shipping / returns information from approved sources
5. FAQ for offer conditions
6. Final CTA

Do not invent countdowns, deadlines, inventory scarcity, original prices, discount stacking, or shipping promises.
Use the actual configured commerce offer rather than typed promotional pricing.
```

## Gift-with-purchase campaign messaging

```text
Update this page to explain the approved gift-with-purchase offer.

Offer:
- qualifying condition: [SPEND / PRODUCT / SUBSCRIPTION CONDITION]
- gift: [GIFT PRODUCT]
- limit: [LIMIT]
- campaign scope: [SCOPE]

Explain the offer in plain language near the purchase decision.
Use the approved gift image if supplied.
Make the condition easy to scan on mobile.

Keep the existing product and purchase behavior unchanged.
Do not create or imply a different qualifying threshold.
Do not claim the gift is free unless the actual Shopify / commerce setup makes it free.
Do not invent inventory or urgency.
```

## Free-shipping campaign messaging

```text
Add clear free-shipping messaging for this approved offer.

Qualification: [MINIMUM AMOUNT / MINIMUM QUANTITY / SUBSCRIPTION CONDITION].
Eligible market / region details: [APPROVED DETAILS].

Place the message where it helps the purchase decision without dominating the page.
Use concise copy and a supporting icon only if it fits the existing design.

Do not change shipping rates or checkout logic.
Do not imply universal free shipping when the real Shopify rule has eligibility restrictions.
```

## Discount-code campaign

```text
Present the approved discount-code campaign on this page.

Code: [CODE]
Approved benefit: [DISCOUNT]
Eligibility: [PRODUCT / PURCHASE TYPE / CUSTOMER CONDITIONS]
Timing: [DATES IF REAL]

Update:
- hero / offer callout;
- purchase-area reminder;
- FAQ if shoppers need to understand restrictions.

Keep the actual code and checkout behavior unchanged.
Do not invent stacking, automatic application, minimum spend, or subscription eligibility.
```

## Subscription-first campaign

```text
Rework the page messaging for a campaign where the preferred purchase is subscription.

Product: [PRODUCT]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Approved subscription benefits: [BENEFITS]
Available plan(s): use the real connected selling plans only.

Explain:
- why subscription fits this customer's use case;
- the approved savings / convenience benefit;
- how often the plan ships where the real plan supplies that information;
- any approved flexibility / cancellation language.

Keep one-time purchase available if it is part of the actual purchase section.
Do not create a selling plan, frequency, discount, cancellation policy, or savings claim that is not in the connected setup / approved source.
```

## One-time purchase–first campaign

```text
The current page over-emphasizes subscription for this campaign.
Reframe the selected purchase story around the one-time purchase while keeping the real subscription option available.

Audience: [AUDIENCE].
Campaign reason: [WHY ONE-TIME IS THE BETTER ENTRY POINT].

Change only the surrounding copy and visual emphasis.
Do not remove real purchase choices or change pricing / plan logic.
```

## Bundle / multi-product campaign brief

```text
Build campaign storytelling around this approved multi-product offer.
Products: [PRODUCTS].
Customer job: [WHAT THE BUNDLE HELPS THEM ACCOMPLISH].
Selection rules / quantities: [REAL RULES].
Approved savings / offer: [REAL OFFER].

Explain why the products belong together and make the selection process easy to understand.
Use the real configured products / quantities and preserve the actual cart behavior.
Do not invent bundle pricing, free items, or automatic discounts.
```

## Creator discount-code campaign

```text
Build / adapt this page for traffic from [CREATOR].
Creator angle: [ANGLE].
Approved code: [CODE].
Approved offer: [OFFER].

Use supplied creator assets and quotes only.
Keep the creator angle visible in the hero and proof sections so the visitor feels continuity from the creator content.
Explain the code clearly near the purchase decision.

Do not invent creator usage, personal results, endorsements, exclusivity, or code eligibility.
```

## Retargeting page

```text
Adapt this page for a warm retargeting audience who has already [VIEWED PRODUCT / VISITED SITE / ENGAGED WITH AD].

Do not repeat basic category education they likely already know.
Prioritize:
- the strongest product differentiator;
- proof / objection handling;
- approved offer if applicable;
- purchase clarity.

Keep the real product and purchase behavior unchanged.
Use only supplied audience information; do not assume a visitor saw a specific page or product unless the campaign targeting guarantees it.
```

## Email / SMS campaign landing page

```text
Build / update this page for traffic from the attached [EMAIL / SMS] campaign.

The message in the campaign is: [MESSAGE].
The offer is: [OFFER].
Audience relationship: [CUSTOMER / SUBSCRIBER / LEAD].

The landing page should not make the customer rediscover the promotion.
Repeat the essential promise immediately, then move into the product / offer details and purchase path.

Keep the campaign terms and destination consistent.
Do not invent subscriber-only benefits or eligibility.
```

## Product launch with no discount

```text
Build a launch page for [PRODUCT] without using a discount as the reason to buy.

Audience: [AUDIENCE].
Launch angle: [ANGLE].
Approved proof: [PROOF].

Focus the page on:
- what is new / relevant;
- the customer problem;
- the product's approved differentiation;
- evidence;
- clear purchase path.

Do not introduce fake urgency, launch pricing, early-bird savings, or scarcity simply because this is a launch.
```

## Present multiple offers without confusing the hierarchy

```text
Review the page's current promotional messages.
Approved offers / benefits are:
1. [OFFER 1]
2. [OFFER 2]
3. [OFFER 3]

Do not edit yet.
Recommend a hierarchy so shoppers understand:
- the primary reason to buy;
- the supporting incentive;
- any conditional offer.

Identify messages that compete or appear contradictory.
Then propose where each message should appear.
Do not change the underlying commerce rules.
```

## Write offer FAQ copy

```text
Create / rewrite FAQ answers for this approved offer.

Offer rules:
[PASTE APPROVED RULES]

Questions should cover only likely purchase confusion, such as:
- who / what qualifies;
- where the discount / gift appears;
- purchase-type eligibility;
- timing;
- shipping or stacking only when supplied.

Keep answers direct and factual.
Do not invent policies or exceptions.
```
