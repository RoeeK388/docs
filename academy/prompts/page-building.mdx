These prompts are for creating a new page or a substantial new section. Attach the real product, creative, approved copy, and references whenever available.

## Product launch landing page

```text
BUSINESS CONTEXT
Launch [PRODUCT] to [AUDIENCE].
Traffic source: [CHANNEL].
Campaign angle: [ANGLE].
Primary action: [ACTION].

REFERENCES
- Attached product = source of real product and purchase information.
- Attached launch creative = message direction.
- AI Knowledge = approved brand voice and claims.

BUILD
Create a launch page with:
1. Message-matched hero
2. Fast proof / credibility
3. Three core benefits
4. Product education / how it works
5. Approved social proof
6. Product / purchase section
7. FAQ focused on launch objections
8. Final CTA

CONSTRAINTS
Do not invent reviews, results, discounts, deadlines, or claims.
Use one primary conversion path.
Prioritize mobile.
```

## Paid-social landing page

```text
Build a dedicated page for cold [META / TIKTOK] traffic.
Audience: [AUDIENCE].
The attached ad is the source of the campaign angle.

The first screen should feel like a natural continuation of that ad:
- same core promise;
- same product emphasis;
- consistent visual mood;
- immediate explanation of what the product is;
- one obvious next action.

Below the fold, use only the education and proof needed to resolve the biggest objections for this audience.
Use the connected product for real commerce information.
Do not turn ad creative into static page artwork when editable content is better.
```

## Advertorial

```text
Create an advertorial for [PRODUCT] aimed at [AUDIENCE] arriving from [CHANNEL / AD ANGLE].

The page should read like a useful editorial story first and a product pitch second.
Structure:
1. Editorial-style headline tied to the problem / curiosity
2. Opening that makes the reader feel understood
3. The problem and why common approaches fall short
4. The relevant mechanism / insight using only approved facts
5. Natural introduction of [PRODUCT]
6. Evidence and approved social proof
7. Clear transition to the purchase section
8. FAQ / objections
9. Final CTA

Use [FOUNDER / EXPERT / CUSTOMER SOURCE] only where actually supplied.
Do not invent editorial endorsements, publications, experts, statistics, or customer outcomes.
Keep the article easy to scan on mobile.
```

## Listicle

```text
Build a listicle landing page for [PRODUCT / CATEGORY].
Audience: [AUDIENCE].
Angle: “[X reasons / mistakes / things to know] about [TOPIC].”

Each list item should:
- make one useful point;
- be understandable without reading every previous item;
- connect naturally to an approved product benefit where relevant;
- use real evidence or supplied imagery;
- avoid repeating the same claim in different words.

After the list, transition into the product / purchase section and answer the main objections.
Use a strong editorial hierarchy, numbered sections, and short mobile paragraphs.
Do not fabricate statistics, expert quotes, reviews, or urgency.
```

## Founder story

```text
Create a founder-story landing page using the supplied founder material.

Goal: make the origin story increase trust and explain why the product exists without turning the page into a biography.

Use this flow:
1. The customer problem the founder saw or experienced
2. Why existing solutions felt insufficient
3. The decision / insight that led to the product
4. What was built and what makes the approach different
5. Approved proof from customers / product facts
6. Product and purchase section
7. Founder-led closing CTA

Use only facts from the supplied founder story and AI Knowledge.
Do not invent hardship, credentials, timelines, press, or motivations.
Keep the customer's problem—not the founder—as the main story.
```

## Creator / UGC landing page

```text
Build a landing page that continues the attached creator ad.
Creator: [CREATOR / TYPE OF CREATOR].
Audience: [AUDIENCE].
Creator angle: [ANGLE].

Keep the page feeling connected to the creator's voice without pretending the entire site is their personal account.

Structure:
1. Hero that repeats the core creator angle
2. Short creator quote / approved UGC moment
3. Why the product fits the creator's routine / use case
4. Product benefits relevant to that audience
5. Additional approved proof
6. Product / purchase section
7. FAQ

Do not invent creator quotes, usage, results, discount codes, or endorsements.
Use the actual creator assets provided.
```

## Comparison / alternative page

```text
Build a comparison page for [PRODUCT] versus [ALTERNATIVE / CATEGORY APPROACH].
Audience: [AUDIENCE].
Decision they are trying to make: [DECISION].

Use a fair comparison framework based only on supplied facts:
- best for;
- key differences;
- product format / experience;
- approved feature / benefit differences;
- purchase model where relevant;
- who should choose which option.

Use a scannable comparison table followed by deeper explanation.
Do not invent competitor pricing, claims, weaknesses, reviews, or legal assertions.
If a fact is not supplied, mark it as something we need to verify instead of guessing.
```

## Build from a screenshot

```text
Reference image 1 is a LAYOUT REFERENCE ONLY.
Recreate the visual structure using editable Jurni content.

Use:
- our brand fonts / colors from AI Knowledge;
- attached product and images;
- approved copy below.

Preserve the reference's:
- information hierarchy;
- section arrangement;
- spacing rhythm;
- broad visual composition.

Do not copy the reference brand's logo, product, reviews, claims, prices, imagery, or exact copy.
Do not place the screenshot itself as the finished section.
```

## Build from a URL

```text
Use [REFERENCE URL] as inspiration for [SPECIFIC SECTIONS / INTERACTION].

Recreate only:
- [HERO STRUCTURE]
- [SECTION / INTERACTION]
- [VISUAL PRINCIPLE]

Use our connected product, brand assets, AI Knowledge, and approved copy.
Do not replicate unrelated sections or copy the other brand's reviews, claims, pricing, imagery, or offer.
Keep all content editable in Jurni.
```

## Build from Figma

```text
Desktop frame: [FIGMA URL]
Mobile frame: [FIGMA URL]

These are two responsive versions of the same page.
Build the page to follow those frames while keeping text, images, buttons, and shopper controls editable.

Use:
- attached product for real commerce information;
- approved assets for media;
- AI Knowledge for brand rules.

Do not add the mobile frame as a second page section.
Where the Figma shows a visual purchasing control, preserve / connect the actual supported purchase behavior rather than making static artwork.
```

## Build a page from an existing Jurni winner

```text
Use [WINNING / APPROVED JURNI PAGE] as the structural starting point for a new campaign.

Keep the proven structure:
- [SECTION ORDER]
- [CTA PATTERN]
- [PROOF PLACEMENT]

Adapt for:
Product: [NEW PRODUCT]
Audience: [NEW AUDIENCE]
Campaign angle: [NEW ANGLE]
Offer: [NEW APPROVED OFFER]

Do not copy product-specific claims, reviews, images, pricing, or purchase settings from the original page unless they also apply to the new product.
```

## Build a pre-launch / waitlist page

```text
Build a pre-launch page for [PRODUCT / LAUNCH].
Primary action: [EMAIL / SMS / WAITLIST].
Audience: [AUDIENCE].
Traffic source: [CHANNEL].

The page should explain enough value to earn the signup without pretending the product is already available.
Include:
1. Clear launch proposition
2. Why this matters to the audience
3. Approved preview / teaser content
4. Proof we can legitimately use before launch
5. Signup form / CTA
6. What happens after signup
7. FAQ about timing / availability using supplied details

Do not invent launch dates, availability, discounts, inventory, testimonials, or guarantees.
```
