Jurni Academy is designed to support **short, task-focused walkthroughs** alongside the written playbooks. The written lesson remains the source of truth so customers can complete the workflow without watching a video.

<Note>
  We are not publishing placeholder or synthetic walkthroughs. Videos will be added here as current Jurni recordings are approved. Until then, use the linked written playbook.
</Note>

## Recommended video format

Each Academy video should be short enough to answer one job clearly—typically **3–8 minutes** for a focused workflow and **10–15 minutes** for a complete launch / experiment walkthrough.

Every recording should show:

1. the marketer goal;
2. what inputs are prepared;
3. the actual Jurni workflow;
4. the prompt used, when AI is involved;
5. the result and what was manually reviewed;
6. the live QA / next action where relevant.

Avoid feature tours that click every control without completing a customer job.

## Recording quality standard

Before publishing a walkthrough:

- use a demo / approved shop with no customer-sensitive data;
- record the **current product UI**, not an old mockup;
- show a real outcome rather than a narrated slide deck when possible;
- keep cursor movement and editing deliberate;
- cut waiting / loading time;
- zoom / crop so the control being discussed is readable;
- show the exact prompt in a copyable form below the video;
- include captions / transcript;
- link to the related Help Center setup article for exact product controls;
- update or retire the recording when the UI materially changes.

## Suggested lesson template

When a recording is added, its Academy page should use:

**Watch** — embedded recording.

**What you'll build** — one-sentence outcome.

**Before you start** — required inputs.

**The prompt** — copy-ready version.

**Key decisions** — what the marketer should notice in the video.

**Launch / test checklist** — manual verification.

**Related Help Center** — exact setup docs.

This prevents the video library from becoming a collection of context-free product demos.
