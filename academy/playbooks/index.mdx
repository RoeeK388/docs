# Jurni Playbooks

Playbooks are **complete workflows**, not feature documentation. Pick the job you want to accomplish and follow the process from inputs to launch or test.

Each playbook gives you:

- when to use the workflow
- the inputs to gather before you build
- a recommended page or campaign structure
- a copy-ready Jurni prompt
- what to review before launch
- the common mistakes that weaken the result
- what to measure or test next

## How to choose the right playbook

Start with the **visitor and campaign**, not the page type you happen to like.

| Situation | Good starting point |
| --- | --- |
| You already have an ad that is working | [Ad → landing page](/academy/playbooks/ad-to-landing-page) |
| You have a strong external page you want to adapt | [Reference-page workflow](/academy/playbooks/recreate-reference-page) |
| Your design team handed you approved frames | [Figma → page](/academy/playbooks/figma-to-page) |
| Your audience needs education before product | [Advertorial](/academy/playbooks/advertorial) |
| The angle works best as several distinct reasons | [Listicle](/academy/playbooks/listicle) |
| Founder credibility is part of the buying reason | [Founder story](/academy/playbooks/founder-story) |
| Traffic comes from a specific creator | [Creator page](/academy/playbooks/creator-page) |
| You already have a page and want to improve it | [CRO review](/academy/playbooks/cro-review) |
| You have a clear hypothesis ready to measure | [First A/B test](/academy/playbooks/first-ab-test) |

Do not choose an advertorial because advertorials are popular. Choose it when the visitor needs **belief-building and explanation** before the purchase decision.

## Playbooks should evolve with evidence

A playbook is a strong starting workflow, not a universal best-performing template. Your audience, category, traffic source, offer, and brand can change what works.

Use Jurni experiments to test the parts of the playbook that are assumptions for your business. Keep the workflow; test the hypothesis.

## Suggest a future playbook

As the library grows, the most useful additions should come from recurring customer workflows: campaigns your team builds often, questions that repeat in support, and patterns that repeatedly produce useful tests.
