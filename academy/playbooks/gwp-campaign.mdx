Use this playbook when the campaign incentive is a **gift with purchase (GWP)** and you need the landing page, Jurni offer rules, and Shopify checkout outcome to agree.

A GWP campaign has two different jobs:

1. **Jurni communicates and handles the supported gift qualification / selection flow.**
2. **Shopify or your commerce setup must actually make the gift free or discounted as promised.**

A beautiful “free gift” banner is not a working GWP campaign if checkout charges for the item.

## Before you start

Define the offer in one sentence:

> When a shopper **[QUALIFYING CONDITION]**, they receive / can choose **[GIFT]**, up to **[LIMIT]**, during **[REAL CAMPAIGN SCOPE / TIMING]**.

Gather:

- gift product / variant;
- qualifying product(s) or threshold;
- purchase-type requirement, if any;
- gift quantity / maximum;
- actual Shopify discount / $0 product setup;
- approved campaign copy / image;
- intended Jurni experiences.

## Phase 1 — Configure the real commerce rule

For the current Shopify flow, use the Jurni **Gift with Purchase** offer to choose the gift and qualifying conditions. Separately configure the actual Shopify pricing / discount setup that makes the gift free when the campaign promises free.

Follow [Set up a gift with purchase](/building-funnels/gift-with-purchase) for the current UI and supported rule types.

Do not use AI copy as a substitute for offer configuration.

## Phase 2 — Write the offer so a shopper can understand it instantly

Good offer copy answers:

- What do I need to do?
- What do I get?
- Is it automatic or do I choose?
- Is there a meaningful limit / condition?

Example:

> Spend $75 and choose a free travel-size [product]. One gift per order.

Avoid vague wording such as “Free gift available” when qualification is conditional.

## Phase 3 — Brief Jurni AI on the campaign messaging

```text
Update this page to communicate the approved gift-with-purchase campaign.

OFFER
Qualifying condition: [CONDITION]
Gift: [GIFT PRODUCT]
Maximum / selection rule: [RULE]
Campaign timing / scope: [REAL DETAILS]

MESSAGE
Make the offer easy to understand near the hero and purchase decision.
Use the approved gift image [ASSET] where helpful.
Explain the qualification in one concise sentence.

KEEP
- existing product / purchase behavior;
- real prices;
- existing cart / checkout action.

DO NOT
- invent a lower threshold;
- claim the gift is free unless the actual Shopify setup makes it free;
- add fake scarcity / inventory;
- imply every page / purchase qualifies when the offer scope is narrower.
```

## Phase 4 — Choose where the offer appears

Useful places:

- hero / campaign announcement when the GWP is a primary reason for the click;
- product / purchase section where the qualification becomes actionable;
- cart / gift-selection flow where the shopper sees qualification status;
- FAQ for conditions that genuinely need explanation.

Avoid repeating the full offer in every section. Repetition can make a simple promotion feel complicated.

## Phase 5 — Test qualification and disqualification

Do not only test the happy path.

For a spend threshold:

1. Cart below threshold → gift should not qualify.
2. Cart reaches threshold → gift becomes available / is added according to setup.
3. Remove qualifying item → gift qualification should update.
4. Add another qualifying item → verify gift quantity / maximum.
5. Proceed to checkout → gift price is correct.

For a specific-product condition:

- test correct product / variant;
- test an unqualified product;
- test one-time vs subscription when purchase type matters;
- test maximum gifts.

## Phase 6 — Check the real cart environment

Jurni-hosted pages and native Shopify Pages can use different cart environments.

If the campaign is published as a Shopify Page or uses a third-party theme cart, verify that the gift flow is supported there; do not assume the Jurni-hosted cart behavior automatically appears in the theme drawer.

Use [Cart & Checkout behavior](/commerce/cart-checkout-behavior) and [Shopify Pages cart connection](/shopify-pages/cart-config) when relevant.

## Phase 7 — QA campaign messaging against the actual rule

Before launch, compare:

| Page says | Commerce must do |
| --- | --- |
| “Free gift over $75” | Gift qualifies at the correct threshold and checkout price is free. |
| “Choose your gift” | Customer actually gets a supported choice rather than an automatic fixed gift. |
| “With subscription” | The real selected selling plan qualifies. |
| “One per order” | Gift quantity / maximum reflects that rule. |

Any mismatch is a launch blocker.

## What good looks like

A shopper should understand the offer without reading fine print, see qualification behave predictably, and reach checkout with the exact gift / price they were promised.

## Common mistakes

### Jurni offer configured, Shopify discount missing

The gift can reach cart at full price.

### Page says “free” because the product is displayed as a gift

Only say free when checkout makes it free.

### Threshold copy differs from the actual rule

Keep one source of truth for the campaign condition.

### Testing only a qualifying cart

Always test losing qualification too.

### Assuming every Shopify Page cart supports the gift UI

Test the actual published environment.

## Useful follow-up tests

Once the offer is working, test **presentation**, not the underlying gift logic:

- offer-led vs product-led hero;
- gift image vs copy-only callout;
- qualification message near hero vs near purchase section;
- fixed gift vs choice of gifts only when both are real campaign options.
