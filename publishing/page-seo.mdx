Use the editor's SEO settings to provide the **title, description, and preview image** for your page. These are separate from the visible headline and images in your design.

## Configure the page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open SEO settings">
    Open the experience, select the **Settings gear**, and find **SEO**. When enabled, choose **Configure SEO**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the page details">
    Set **Title**, **Description**, and **Preview Image**. Use wording and an image that match this campaign and product. Preview Image takes an image URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the previews">
    Check the Google Search and Social Media previews in the dialog. The title and description counters help you review their length; they are not performance scores.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and verify">
    Save the form, check the page's publishing state, and test the actual destination you will share. For a native Shopify Page, check the Shopify URL too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Enable SEO** saves its configuration change directly, while the details form has its own save action. Do not assume these settings stay private until you publish the page design.
</Note>

## What should I enter?

Use a title that identifies the page's product or purpose, a description that summarizes the offer accurately, and a preview image that still makes sense outside the page.

For example, a seasonal landing page should not retain a previous campaign's promotion in its description. After duplicating a page, review these fields as well as the visible copy.

## Important distinctions

The preview image is not the image shown in your ad, and editing these fields does not update the ad creative. The previews help you review your settings; they do not guarantee how a search engine or sharing platform will display the page.

SEO settings are also not access controls. Do not use them as a way to keep a page confidential. For review access, use [Share and review links](/build/share-review-links).
