Jurni Shopify Pages are published into the connected Shopify store and follow the domain structure of that Shopify instance. This is different from Jurni-hosted experiences, where you can use a dedicated custom subdomain connected to Jurni.

## Current limitation

A single Jurni Shopify Pages setup does not support publishing pages across multiple independent **root domains** inside the same Shopify instance as separate Jurni publishing destinations.

For example:

- `brand.com` and `go.brand.com` are part of the same root domain family.
- `brand.com` and `anotherbrand.com` are separate root domains.

<Warning>
If your Shopify store serves multiple root domains, do not assume a Jurni Shopify Page configured for one root domain can automatically be published and managed on the other domain.
</Warning>

## What you can do instead

Depending on the use case, consider:

1. **Use a Jurni custom subdomain** for the additional campaign domain.
2. **Keep the Shopify Page on the primary Shopify domain** and route campaign traffic there.
3. **Use Smart Links** when the main requirement is a stable campaign URL with flexible routing.
4. Contact your Jurni team when the store has a non-standard multi-domain architecture so the implementation can be reviewed before launch.

## Subdomains are different

Using a campaign subdomain such as `go.brand.com` or `try.brand.com` is a different setup from adding a second root domain. Custom subdomains can be connected to Jurni and used to host campaign experiences independently of Shopify Pages.
