**Publishing makes a page available to shoppers. A Smart Link determines where your campaign traffic goes.** You can keep the campaign link the same while changing its destination in Jurni.

## Understand the URLs

These examples show the different paths for a standard Shopify Online Store. Use the actual URLs provided in your account.

| Publishing path | Example | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Jurni custom subdomain** | `go.brand.com/summer-offer` | A branded Jurni-hosted page or Smart Link on your connected subdomain. |
| **Shopify app proxy** | `brand.com/apps/jurni/summer-offer` | The default Jurni route for Shopify stores without a connected custom subdomain. It is not a native Shopify Page. |
| **Shopify Pages** | `brand.com/pages/summer-offer` | A page built in Jurni and published into the Shopify Online Store's theme environment. |

A URL on your store's root domain does not, by itself, tell you which publishing path is in use. Check whether it is the app-proxy route or a native Shopify Page.

## Follow the path for your storefront

**Shopify Online Store:** use the Jurni-hosted flow, with the app-proxy route available before a custom subdomain is connected. Use Shopify Pages when you specifically want a native `/pages/` destination and the Shopify theme environment.

**Shopify headless or another commerce platform:** use the domain, cart, checkout, and tracking arrangement agreed for that storefront. Installing a Shopify theme block does not install it on a separate headless frontend. Native Shopify Pages require a Shopify Online Store theme; they are not a publishing mechanism for a WooCommerce or Salesforce storefront.

## Publish, then connect the campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the page">
    Finish the design and product setup. Check mobile and desktop and save the work you intend to review. A preview link is not your live campaign URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish to the intended destination">
    Open the editor's publishing controls. For a native Shopify Page, complete its [template setup and Shopify publication](/shopify-pages/setup-publish).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the campaign entry link">
    Use a [Smart Link](/building-funnels/smart-links) for a destination you want to manage centrally. For an experiment, use the link connected through the [test setup](/experiments/create-experiment).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the live journey">
    Open the exact campaign link. Check the destination, product options, cart, checkout, tracking, and any forms or reviews before sending visitors.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Moving to Shopify Pages

A Shopify Page uses a different theme and cart environment from a Jurni-hosted page. Check the [limitations](/shopify-pages/limitations) and [cart setup](/shopify-pages/cart-config) before switching a live campaign. Do not assume a custom script or widget that works in one environment works unchanged in the other.

## After launch

Use [Update or unpublish](/publishing/update-unpublish) when changing page content or retiring a destination. Use [Change a Smart Link destination](/publishing/change-smart-link-destination) when changing where the campaign sends visitors.

For the full launch check, follow [Test your campaign link](/publishing/check-campaign-link).
