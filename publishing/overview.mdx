Publishing answers two separate questions:

1. **Where does the page live?**
2. **What URL should the campaign use to send traffic there?**

Those are often related, but they are not the same thing. A page can be published at one destination while your campaign uses a Smart Link that routes visitors to it.

## Choose where the page should live

Use this as the starting decision:

| Your situation | Recommended path to understand first | What it means for the marketer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shopify brand using Jurni as the campaign-page environment** | Jurni-hosted page / custom subdomain | Jurni serves the page. Your custom subdomain gives it a branded campaign URL. Before a subdomain is connected, Shopify shops can use the Jurni app-proxy route. |
| **Shopify brand that specifically needs the page inside the Shopify theme** | Shopify Pages | Jurni publishes the page into a native `/pages/` destination. Theme, cart, and app behavior need to be checked in that Shopify environment. |
| **Shopify headless storefront** | Your headless publishing + commerce setup | The Shopify backend does not mean a Shopify theme is present. Use the domain, cart, checkout, and tracking implementation agreed for the headless storefront. |
| **WooCommerce, Salesforce, BigCommerce, or another custom storefront** | Your connected / custom publishing setup | Jurni works with the storefront and purchase flow agreed during implementation; Shopify Pages instructions do not apply. |

If you are unsure which one your campaign should use, start with [Supported commerce platforms](/getting-started/choose-your-setup) or ask the Jurni team before moving a live URL.

## Jurni-hosted pages

For a Shopify Online Store, Jurni can serve the campaign page outside the native Shopify theme while still connecting to the Shopify commerce flow.

**Without a custom subdomain**, the default Shopify route is typically an app-proxy URL such as:

`brand.com/apps/jurni/summer-offer`

**With a custom subdomain**, you can use a branded Jurni domain such as:

`go.brand.com/summer-offer`

A custom subdomain is usually the cleaner campaign-facing setup when your team wants branded hosted URLs. See [Set up a custom subdomain](/getting-started/custom-subdomain).

## Shopify Pages

A Shopify Page is a different publishing environment. The destination looks like:

`brand.com/pages/summer-offer`

Choose this path because you intentionally want the page in the Shopify Online Store theme—not simply because `/pages/` looks more familiar.

Before using it for a live campaign, verify:

- the Jurni page template / app block
- theme layout and page width
- existing theme scripts and apps
- the theme or cart-app connection
- forms / review widgets used by the campaign
- the final purchase path

A widget or script that works on a Jurni-hosted page does not automatically behave identically inside the Shopify theme. Follow [Publish to Shopify Pages](/shopify-pages/setup-publish), [Shopify Pages limitations](/shopify-pages/limitations), and [cart connection](/shopify-pages/cart-config).

## Headless and non-Shopify destinations

Do not try to translate the Shopify instructions word for word.

Your team may use:

- a Jurni-hosted campaign page connected to your commerce flow
- your existing storefront destination
- a custom/headless route
- an existing product page
- a direct checkout or custom purchase path

The exact domain and purchase handoff depend on the implementation agreed for that storefront. See [Headless and non-Shopify purchase paths](/commerce/non-shopify-purchase-flows).

## Then choose the URL your campaign will actually use

A **Smart Link** is a stable campaign-facing Jurni URL whose destination can be managed in Jurni. It is especially useful when:

- paid media should use one stable URL
- you may change the destination later
- the campaign will be routed into an experiment

For an experiment, the Smart Link is the traffic entry point Jurni uses to split visitors across variants.

You do not have to call every published page through a Smart Link. The important thing is to know whether you are testing the **page URL** or the **campaign URL**.

## The launch workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Finish and save the page">
    Review the campaign message, product/action, desktop, and mobile. Save the work you intend to publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish to the intended environment">
    Publish through the Jurni-hosted path, native Shopify Pages, or the custom storefront implementation that applies to your shop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or confirm the campaign URL">
    If the campaign uses a Smart Link, connect it to the intended page or experiment. Copy the exact URL your ads, email, creator, or other traffic source will use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open that exact URL">
    Do not stop at Preview. Test the campaign URL from a clean browser path and on mobile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the important action">
    Check product choices, form submission, cart / checkout, offer, integrations, and tracking that matter to this campaign.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Which URL should I send to Meta or TikTok?

Use the URL you intentionally chose as the **campaign entry point**. When you want Jurni to manage routing or run an experiment, that is typically the relevant Smart Link—not the editor Preview URL.

If a campaign is already live, understand what currently receives traffic before changing the destination or connecting its Smart Link to a new experiment.

## Before moving an existing campaign to another publishing path

Moving from Jurni-hosted to Shopify Pages is not just a URL change. You are also moving into a different theme/cart/script environment. Re-test the full campaign before switching traffic.

Similarly, do not assume a root-domain page and subdomain page use identical third-party attribution behavior. Verify the tools that matter to the campaign.
