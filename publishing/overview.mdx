Publishing makes a Jurni experience available to real shoppers. Jurni supports different publishing paths so you can choose the URL and storefront setup that fits the campaign.

## Publishing options

The two primary publishing models are:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Jurni-hosted or custom subdomain" icon="globe">
    Publish the experience through Jurni and serve it from the Jurni URL or a custom subdomain connected to your workspace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shopify Pages" icon="shop">
    Publish the experience as a native page inside your Shopify store, typically under your store's `/pages/` path.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Choose the right publishing method

| Need | Recommended path |
| --- | --- |
| Launch a dedicated paid-traffic landing page quickly | Jurni-hosted/custom subdomain |
| Use a URL on the Shopify root domain | Shopify Pages |
| Keep the page inside the Shopify theme environment | Shopify Pages |
| Route ad traffic through Smart Links | Either publishing method |
| Use a branded campaign subdomain | Custom subdomain |

<Info>
Your publishing method does not determine whether you can use Jurni experiments or Smart Links. Those traffic tools can route shoppers to supported published destinations.
</Info>

## Before you publish

Run a full QA pass before sending traffic:

- Preview desktop and mobile layouts.
- Test every important CTA.
- Add products and variants to cart.
- Test subscription or bundle options.
- Confirm tracking pixels and custom scripts are configured correctly.
- Check the final checkout path.
- Verify the page URL and any Smart Link routing.

## Publishing and draft changes

Editing an experience does not mean every change should immediately be visible to shoppers. Use the publish/update flow to control when your latest version becomes live.

For a live campaign, make important changes deliberately and test them before publishing the new version.

## Publishing with experiments

If an experience is part of an experiment, make sure the variant you intend to use is published and available before routing meaningful traffic to it. Draft or unavailable destinations can affect the shopper experience and the validity of test results.
