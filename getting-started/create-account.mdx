Your Jurni account gives you access to one or more Jurni shops/workspaces. After you have access, complete the shop setup before building or sending traffic to an experience.

## Create or join your Jurni account

Use the invitation or onboarding link provided for your Jurni account. Sign in with the email address that should own your Jurni user profile.

If your team already has a Jurni shop, ask an existing admin to invite you rather than creating a separate workspace for the same store.

## Account vs. shop

It is helpful to separate two concepts:

- **Your user account** is your personal Jurni login.
- **A shop/workspace** contains the brand's products, experiences, integrations, analytics, and team access.

One person can have access to more than one shop.

## Initial shop setup

After entering the shop, work through these areas before launch:

1. Connect the correct Shopify store or supported commerce setup.
2. Activate Jurni Core for Shopify where required.
3. Configure Brand Settings.
4. Add AI Knowledge.
5. Connect required tracking and review integrations.
6. Invite teammates.
7. Connect a custom subdomain or configure Shopify Pages if required by the campaign.

## Invite teammates instead of sharing credentials

Every teammate should use their own Jurni user. Do not share one login across the team.

Separate user access makes it easier to manage permissions, collaboration, and account ownership as the team changes.

## If you cannot see the expected shop

Check:

- You signed in with the email address that was invited.
- The invitation was sent to the correct email.
- You are not signed into a second Jurni account in another browser/profile.
- An admin has actually added you to the intended shop.
