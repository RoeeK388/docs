Jurni makes it easy to collaborate with your team by inviting members to your workspace and assigning roles that match their access needs. This guide walks you through viewing current members, sending invitations, understanding role permissions, and managing pending invites.

## Accessing Team Members

Follow these steps to open the Members page and see who has access to your workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Jurni">
    Open Jurni and sign in to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Members">
    Click **Members**. You will see a list of current team members and any pending invitations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Inviting a New Team Member

Send an invitation to add someone to your workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Invite Member">
    Click **Invite Member** in the top-right corner of the Members page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email">
    Type the team member's email address into the invitation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a role">
    Choose either **Admin** or **Staff** based on the level of access they need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Send**. The invited user will receive an email with instructions to join your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Invited users must accept the invitation via the email they receive before they can access your workspace.
</Note>

## Team Roles and Permissions

Jurni offers two roles so you can balance collaboration with security. Choose the role that matches what each team member needs to do.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="shield-halved">
    Full workspace control, including billing and member management.

    **Can do:**

    - Use all product features
    - Manage billing and subscription settings
    - Invite and manage team members
    - Change workspace and account-level settings
  </Card>

  <Card title="Staff" icon="user">
    Day-to-day product access without sensitive administrative controls.

    **Can do:**

    - Create and edit experiences
    - Run experiments
    - View analytics and performance data

    **Cannot do:**

    - Access billing or subscription settings
    - Invite or remove users
    - Change account-level permissions
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Managing Pending Invitations

When you invite someone, they appear in the members list with a **Pending Invitation** status until they accept.

- You can review all pending invitations on the Members page.
- If a user has not accepted, you can resend the invitation so they receive a fresh email with join instructions.

<Note>
  Pending invitations do not count as active seats until the invited user accepts and joins the workspace.
</Note>
