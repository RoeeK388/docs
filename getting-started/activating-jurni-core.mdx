Jurni Core connects your Shopify store to Jurni funnels so you can track sessions, attribute purchases, and power analytics. If your store runs on Shopify, this is a required onboarding step. Without it, Jurni cannot accurately track conversions or attribute performance to your funnels and campaigns.

<Warning>
  If you skip this step, tracking and attribution will be incomplete. You will not be able to accurately measure funnel performance, campaign ROI, or experiment results.
</Warning>

## When to activate Jurni Core

Activate Jurni Core if any of the following apply:

- Your store is on Shopify.
- You want accurate funnel analytics and attribution.
- You plan to launch funnels, run experiments, or send paid traffic.

You only need to complete this activation once per Shopify store.

## How to activate Jurni Core

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open activation settings">
    Log in to Jurni, go to **Settings**, then click **Account**. Locate the **Activate Jurni Core** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the Shopify connection">
    Click **Activate & Open**. You will be redirected to your Shopify admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the app in your theme">
    In the Shopify theme editor, enable the Jurni Core app in your active theme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and return">
    Save your changes in Shopify. Once enabled, you will be redirected back to Jurni and the status will update to **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Jurni Core enables

After activation, Jurni Core provides the following capabilities:

- Accurate session and funnel traffic tracking
- Purchase attribution to funnels and campaigns
- Analytics, experiments, and reporting powered by Shopify checkout events
- Connection of Shopify checkout events back to Jurni

## Troubleshooting

If Jurni Core still shows as **Disabled** after activation:

1. Confirm the app is enabled in your active Shopify theme.
2. Make sure you saved changes in the theme editor.
3. If the issue persists, contact [support@getjurni.ai](mailto:support@getjurni.ai).
