## Jurni works with your existing commerce setup

**Shopify has a native Jurni integration out of the box.** Connect your existing Shopify store during onboarding; you do not need to rebuild your store to start using Jurni.

Jurni also supports working with **headless storefronts, custom storefronts, WooCommerce, Salesforce, BigCommerce, and other commerce setups**. These use an implementation appropriate to your existing platform. The Jurni team confirms how product data, cart and checkout, tracking, and purchases connect before launch.

These are not interchangeable setup options you need to choose between. Your current storefront and commerce provider determine the integration work. See [Supported commerce platforms](/getting-started/choose-your-setup) for what applies to your store.

<Note>
  Already completed onboarding? Go straight to **Build** or the feature you need. You do not need to repeat account creation to follow an editor guide.
</Note>

## Understand the main parts

- **Performance and paid media teams** who need to launch agentic shopping journeys at the speed of their ad sets, without waiting on design or dev
- **CMOs and VP Marketing** managing multiple channels and brand lines who need one engine for performance, discovery, and AI discoverability
- **E-commerce directors and brand owners** who want to remove backlog dependency entirely and launch the right journey today, not weeks

## How Jurni Works: 

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Shop** | The brand/store you work in, with its products, experiences, integrations, team access, and reporting. |
| **Experience** | The shopping experience you create and manage in Jurni. |
| **Page** | A shopper-facing page within that experience or a published destination. |
| **Section** | A part of a page, such as a hero, review block, FAQ, or Buy Box. |
| **Buy Box** | The purchasing interface where shoppers select a product or purchase option and continue to cart or checkout. |
| **Smart Link** | A campaign-facing URL whose destination is managed in Jurni. |
| **Experiment** | A test that assigns incoming traffic to variants so you can compare results. |

## Before sending your first campaign

Finish the [first-launch checklist](/getting-started/step-by-step-setup), review the page on desktop and mobile, and test the actual purchase path. A good-looking preview is not a substitute for checking the final live URL, tracking, and checkout.
