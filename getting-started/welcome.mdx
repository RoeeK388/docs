Jurni helps growth and ecommerce teams turn a campaign idea into a shopper experience, launch it through the right URL, and learn what to improve next.

The easiest way to understand Jurni is as one connected workflow—not a collection of separate features.

## The Jurni campaign workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your shop">
    Start with the commerce setup you already use. Shopify connects natively; headless and non-Shopify storefronts use the product, cart, checkout, and tracking integration agreed for that storefront.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give Jurni your campaign context">
    Set up your brand and AI Knowledge, then bring the real campaign inputs into Jurni: your product, ad creative, imagery, approved copy, Figma frames, or reference pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the experience">
    Use Jurni AI for the first build and larger creative changes, then use the editor for precise adjustments. Your page should continue the message that earned the click and make the next customer decision clear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the real action">
    Make sure the page uses the correct product, form, CTA destination, or purchase path. A button that looks right is not enough if it sends the shopper to the wrong place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and choose the campaign URL">
    Publish to the destination that fits your storefront, then use the correct campaign URL. Smart Links let you manage a campaign destination centrally and are also the entry point for Jurni experiments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the full journey">
    Open the exact URL your audience will click. Check mobile, product selections, offer, cart or checkout, forms, integrations, and tracking—not only the editor preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn and iterate">
    Review experience analytics or run an experiment around one clear hypothesis. Keep what you learn and use it to improve the next page or test.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Jurni works with your existing commerce setup

**Shopify:** connect the existing store and use Jurni with its product and checkout ecosystem. Depending on the campaign, you can publish through Jurni-hosted routes or intentionally publish a native Shopify Page.

**Headless or non-Shopify:** keep the storefront and commerce system you already use. The Jurni team confirms how product data, cart / checkout, and purchase tracking connect. See [Supported commerce platforms](/getting-started/choose-your-setup).

You do not need to choose a platform because Jurni has several options—the platform you already operate determines the right implementation.

## The few Jurni concepts worth knowing

| Term | Think of it as… |
| --- | --- |
| **Experience** | The page / shopper journey your team creates and manages in Jurni. |
| **Section** | A part of the page, such as the hero, proof, FAQ, or purchase area. |
| **Smart Link** | The campaign-facing URL whose destination Jurni can manage. |
| **Experiment** | A test that uses a Smart Link to assign incoming traffic across variants. |
| **Analytics** | The view of sessions, shopper actions, orders, and revenue associated with Jurni experiences and experiments. |

You can learn the product one task at a time. You do not need to memorize every term before building.
