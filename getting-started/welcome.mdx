## What Is Jurni?

Jurni is the Agentic Commerce Engine for scaling DTC brands, giving you one engine to generate personalized agentic shopping journeys across performance and discovery. From paid traffic to AI-driven product discovery, Jurni connects conversion-focused agentic shopping journeys your brand converts better today. No designers. No developers. No waiting.

---

## Who Jurni Is Built For?

- **Performance and paid media teams** who need to launch agentic shopping journeys at the speed of their ad sets, without waiting on design or dev
- **CMOs and VP Marketing** managing multiple channels and brand lines who need one engine for performance, discovery, and AI discoverability
- **E-commerce directors and brand owners** who want to remove backlog dependency entirely and launch the right journey today, not weeks

---

## What Makes Jurni Different?

### Built for Performance and Discovery

Jurni generates agentic shopping journeys across both critical goals:

- Performance journeys matched to paid traffic from Meta, Google, TikTok, and beyond
- Discovery journeys structured for AI search surfaces, so your brand gets recommended when shoppers ask ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity what to buy

### AI Discovery

Most brands are invisible to AI search. Their content isn't structured for how LLMs read, rank, and recommend.

Jurni fixes that. The engine generates AEO-optimized content journeys built for LLM consumption, so when a shopper asks an AI for a recommendation in your category, your brand shows up. The brands that get there first win the channel.

### No Code Required

Build landing pages, multi-step funnels, and content experiences using a visual editor. No developers needed.

### Built for Testing

Run experiments, split traffic between variants, and identify winning funnels directly inside the platform.

### Native Commerce Integrations

Connect directly with Shopify for products and checkout, and integrate with tools like Meta Ads, Google Ads, and Klaviyo.

### Smart Traffic Routing

Use Smart Links to route traffic to funnels and change destinations at any time—without updating live ads or URLs.

1. **Build**<br />Create funnels using AI or templates—for conversion or discovery.
2. **Connect**<br />Link funnels to campaigns, Smart Links, or AI discovery surfaces.
3. **Test**<br />Run experiments to compare messaging, layouts, and offers.
4. **Optimize**<br />Use performance and discovery insights to improve results.
5. **Scale**<br />Deploy winning funnels across paid traffic and AI-driven channels.

## How the AI Engine Works To Build Pages:

1. **Ingests your brand data**, including persona segments, branding, and campaign context
2. **Connects to your ad** and analyzes the creative, angle, and audience
3. **Asks you one question**: What do you want to build?
4. **Delivers a fully built agentic shopping journey**, ready in minutes
