Jurni helps ecommerce teams build, publish, test, and optimize shoppable landing experiences without moving every campaign through a traditional design and development cycle.

This documentation is organized around the jobs you perform in Jurni rather than around internal product names.

## Start here if you're new

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your workspace">

## What you can build

Jurni is used for experiences such as:

- Paid-media landing pages
- Product detail and offer pages
- Advertorials and listicles
- Creator and influencer pages
- Bundle and build-your-own-bundle experiences
- Gifting and seasonal pages
- Product launches
- Quiz or multi-step shopping journeys
- Campaign-specific Shopify Pages

The right structure depends on the campaign. A single focused page can be more effective than adding extra funnel steps that the shopper does not need.

## Commerce

Jurni connects the page experience with your commerce setup so shoppers can interact with real products and continue into cart and checkout.

Core commerce concepts include:

- Products and variants
- Buy Boxes
- One-time and subscription purchase options
- Bundles
- Gift with purchase
- Shopify discounts and promotions
- Cart and checkout behavior

## Publishing

Choose between:

- **Jurni-hosted/custom subdomain:** useful for dedicated campaign landing pages.
- **Shopify Pages:** useful when the page should live on the Shopify root domain and inside a Shopify page template.

You can also use Smart Links when you want a stable campaign URL with flexible routing behind it.

## Testing and analytics

Jurni lets you evaluate more than the final purchase. Use sessions, add-to-cart, checkout, CVR, AOV, revenue, and revenue per session to understand where performance changes.

When you need a controlled comparison, create an experiment instead of comparing unrelated traffic periods.

## Integrations

Connect the tools your team already uses for analytics, advertising, reviews, collaboration, and custom tracking. Common setups include Meta, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, review platforms, Slack, and custom scripts.
