## Jurni works with your existing storefront

### Shopify Online Store

Jurni connects directly to the Shopify store and can use the connected product catalog, checkout / commerce setup, and Shopify-specific publishing options.

Depending on the campaign, a page can run in the Jurni-hosted environment or be published as a native Shopify Page.

[Shopify setup →](/getting-started/shopify-setup-overview)

### Headless and non-Shopify

Jurni can also work with headless storefronts and other commerce platforms. The implementation depends on the store's existing product, cart, checkout, tracking, and URL architecture.

The team may connect the campaign through a cart API, checkout link, existing PDP, or another custom route rather than applying Shopify instructions to a different storefront.

[Choose your setup →](/getting-started/choose-your-setup)

## The Jurni campaign model

You do not need to memorize internal product architecture. For a marketer, the main flow is:

**Campaign idea → Experience → Published page → Smart Link → Shopper journey → Analytics / Experiment → Next iteration**

### Experience

The page / journey your team builds and manages in Jurni.

### Page / section

The shopper-facing content. Sections are parts of the page such as a hero, proof block, FAQ, or purchasing area.

### Smart Link

The campaign-facing URL whose destination is managed in Jurni. It can send traffic to a page or become the entry point for an experiment.

### Experiment

A comparison that assigns Smart Link traffic between variants so your team can learn from the same campaign entry point.

### Shop

The brand / store workspace that holds its products, experiences, integrations, access, and reporting.

## Before your first live campaign

Do not stop at Preview.

Check the actual campaign path:

1. page design on mobile / desktop;
2. product / conversion action;
3. real offer / cart / checkout;
4. forms / reviews / integrations;
5. consent and tracking;
6. the exact Smart Link or campaign URL you will put in ads.

Use [Check your campaign link](/publishing/check-campaign-link) before traffic starts.
