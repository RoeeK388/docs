Jurni is organized around one continuous workflow: create the right experience for a campaign, connect it to the real customer action, launch it through the right URL, and use performance data to improve it.

## The main parts of Jurni

### Experiences

An experience is the page or journey you create in Jurni. You can start with AI, a template, an existing page, or a blank canvas and then refine every section in the editor.

### Products and commerce

Connect the products, variants, selling plans, bundles, offers, forms, or destinations the customer should interact with. The available setup depends on whether you use Shopify, a headless storefront, or another commerce platform.

### Publishing and Smart Links

Publishing makes an experience available at a live URL. A Smart Link is a campaign-facing link whose destination you can manage in Jurni without repeatedly changing the link in your ads or campaigns.

### Tests and analytics

Tests use a Smart Link to split incoming traffic between variants. Experience and test reporting help you compare customer behavior, orders, revenue, and other available metrics.

## A typical campaign in Jurni

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the experience">
    Start from your campaign brief, ad creative, product, offer, or reference page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect the customer action">
    Confirm the product, cart, checkout, form, or destination behind every important CTA.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish and verify">
    Publish using the appropriate setup and test the full customer journey on mobile and desktop.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send traffic">
    Use the live page URL or a Smart Link in your campaign.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and improve">
    Use reporting or a focused test to decide what should change next.
  </Step>
</Steps>
