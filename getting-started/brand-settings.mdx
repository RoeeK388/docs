Brand Settings give Jurni a reusable source of truth for the visual identity and product context your team uses when creating experiences. Configure these settings before generating or templating a large number of pages so new work starts closer to your brand by default.

## What belongs in Brand Settings

Depending on your workspace configuration, Brand Settings can include information such as:

- Brand logo
- Brand typography / fonts
- Core visual identity settings
- Product mapping or product context used by Jurni

Brand Settings are different from **AI Knowledge**. Brand Settings define reusable brand and product configuration, while AI Knowledge provides additional information Jurni can use when generating or reasoning about content.

## Logo

Upload the primary logo you want Jurni to use as the default brand reference.

For the cleanest results:

- Use a high-quality source file.
- Prefer a transparent background when the logo will appear over different page colors.
- Make sure the logo remains readable at mobile sizes.
- Keep alternate campaign logos inside the experience when they should not become the workspace default.

## Fonts

Configure the fonts that represent your brand's primary typography. Consistent workspace-level fonts make AI-generated pages, templates, and newly added sections easier to keep on brand.

<Tip>
  After adding a custom font, preview important pages on both desktop and mobile. Font files and fallback behavior can affect wrapping, spacing, and section height.
</Tip>

## Product mapping

Product mapping helps Jurni understand which commerce products should be associated with the workspace or brand context used during creation.

Keep the mapped products current when:

- New hero products launch.
- A product is discontinued.
- Variants or pack sizes change materially.
- The team changes which products should be prioritized in generated experiences.

## Brand Settings and existing pages

Treat Brand Settings as a default source for creation—not as a substitute for QA. When you change brand configuration, review important existing experiences rather than assuming every previously customized element will update automatically.

## Brand Settings and AI generation

The better the brand foundation, the less cleanup your team should need after AI generation. Before generating a campaign page, make sure the workspace contains the correct logo, typography, product context, and AI Knowledge for that campaign.

## Keep campaign-specific choices on the page

Not every visual decision belongs in global Brand Settings. Temporary campaign colors, seasonal art direction, one-off logos, or experimental typography are usually better configured in the experience itself so they do not become the default for unrelated pages.
