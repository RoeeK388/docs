AI Knowledge gives Jurni additional brand and product context to use when generating, editing, or reasoning about experiences. It is most valuable when the information is accurate, specific, and reusable across campaigns.

## AI Knowledge vs. Brand Settings

Use the two areas for different jobs:

| Brand Settings | AI Knowledge |
| --- | --- |
| Visual identity and reusable brand configuration | Deeper product, audience, positioning, and brand context |
| Logos and typography | Product facts and differentiators |
| Product mapping / default product context | Messaging guidance and approved claims |
| Defaults used during creation | Knowledge Jurni can use while generating or assisting |

## What to add

Prioritize information that helps Jurni make better content decisions, such as:

- Product benefits and differentiators
- Approved product facts
- Ingredient, material, or technical context
- Target audiences and customer pain points
- Brand voice and messaging guidance
- Frequently asked product questions
- Important positioning or competitive context
- Claims the brand is approved to make

## What not to use as evergreen knowledge

Avoid filling the knowledge base with temporary or contradictory campaign instructions.

Examples that are usually better kept in the specific page or prompt:

- A one-week discount
- A temporary holiday headline
- A campaign-specific promo code
- Experimental messaging that has not been approved
- Outdated product information

<Warning>
  AI Knowledge should not become a dumping ground for every internal document. Incorrect or conflicting information makes generated output less reliable.
</Warning>

## Set up AI Knowledge

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Knowledge">
    Go to the AI Knowledge area for your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add verified information">
    Add the source material or knowledge entries you want Jurni to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize around reusable concepts">
    Keep product facts, audience context, brand guidance, and other reusable information easy to distinguish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review for conflicts">
    Remove outdated or duplicated information that could give the AI competing instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with a real build">
    Generate or edit an experience and confirm Jurni is using the knowledge in the way you expect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep knowledge current

Review AI Knowledge whenever the brand changes something fundamental:

- New product launch
- Formula or ingredient change
- Rebrand
- New approved claims
- New target audience
- Major pricing or positioning change

## Use prompts for campaign instructions

AI Knowledge should provide the stable background. The prompt should describe what you want **for this campaign**.

For example, keep evergreen product benefits in AI Knowledge, then prompt Jurni to build a creator-style landing page for a specific Meta ad angle.
