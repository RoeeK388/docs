## Which setup do I have?

| Your storefront | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Customers browse a standard Shopify theme | [Shopify setup overview](/getting-started/shopify-setup-overview) |
| Shopify manages products and checkout, but your frontend is custom | [Shopify headless setup](/getting-started/shopify-headless) |
| Your products or checkout are outside Shopify | [Non-Shopify setup](/getting-started/setup-non-shopify) |

## What changes between setups?

The Jurni building experience is broadly the same. The main differences are how product data reaches Jurni, what happens when a customer adds to cart or checks out, where the page is published, and how purchase tracking is confirmed.

<Note>
  A live landing-page URL does not by itself confirm that products, checkout, or purchase measurement are connected. Complete the setup guide for your storefront before sending campaign traffic.
</Note>
