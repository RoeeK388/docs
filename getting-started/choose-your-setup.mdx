Your Jurni setup depends on **where your storefront runs**, not only which commerce platform processes the order.

Use this guide before configuring publishing, tracking, or checkout behavior.

## Shopify Online Store

Choose this path when your customer-facing storefront uses a standard Shopify Online Store theme.

Jurni integrates with the Shopify store and **Jurni Core** can run through the Shopify theme/app environment for session, visit, cart, and attribution tracking.

For publishing, Shopify brands have three relevant URL patterns:

1. **Jurni page on your Shopify app proxy** — the default Shopify-hosted route when no custom Jurni subdomain is configured, using a URL such as `yourdomain.com/apps/jurni/<handle>`.
2. **Jurni page on a custom subdomain** — after you connect a branded subdomain, Jurni pages and Smart Links can use that domain.
3. **Native Shopify Page** — an optional publishing method that places the Jurni page inside a Shopify page template at `yourdomain.com/pages/<handle>`.

<Info>
Native Shopify Pages are an additional publishing option for Shopify Online Store brands. They are not required in order to use Jurni.
</Info>

Start with [Shopify Setup](/getting-started/shopify-setup-overview), then review [Publishing Jurni Pages](/publishing/overview).

## Shopify Headless

Choose this path when Shopify is still your commerce backend but the shopper-facing site is **not rendered by the Shopify Online Store theme**.

In a headless setup, Jurni cannot rely on the standard Shopify theme-extension path. The Jurni tracking script is loaded directly on the headless storefront and can use host-specific configuration for the domains connected to the shop.

Shopify's cart/order data can still be used for attribution, but the integration needs to preserve the Jurni session/visit and cart or checkout signals through the custom storefront flow.

<Warning>
Do not follow the native Shopify Pages instructions for a headless storefront. Shopify Pages rely on Shopify theme templates and the Jurni app block.
</Warning>

Start with [Shopify Headless Setup](/getting-started/shopify-headless).

## Non-Shopify or custom commerce

Choose this path when the storefront and commerce flow are outside Shopify.

Jurni can load its browser-side tracking directly on a custom storefront, but commerce events, cart/checkout behavior, and order attribution depend on the APIs and event sources available in your stack.

Because these implementations are not all identical, confirm the required integration with the Jurni team before launching paid traffic.

Start with [Non-Shopify Setup](/getting-started/setup-non-shopify).

## Quick comparison

| Setup | Storefront | Jurni Core / tracking | Publishing options |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shopify Online Store** | Shopify theme | Shopify integration / theme environment | App proxy, custom Jurni subdomain, or native Shopify Pages |
| **Shopify Headless** | Custom/headless frontend with Shopify commerce | Jurni script loaded directly on the storefront | Jurni-hosted/custom-domain or implementation-specific flow; not Shopify Pages |
| **Non-Shopify** | Custom/non-Shopify storefront | Jurni script + custom event/commerce integration | Jurni-hosted/custom-domain or implementation-specific flow |
