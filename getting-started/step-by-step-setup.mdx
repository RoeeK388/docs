This quickstart gives you the shortest path from a campaign idea to a live Jurni experience.

You do not need to configure every feature before you begin. Start with one real product, offer, or campaign and complete the essential path below.

<Note>
  If your Jurni shop is not connected yet, first [choose the setup for your commerce platform](/getting-started/choose-your-setup).
</Note>

## Before you begin

Have these campaign inputs ready:

- The product, offer, or action the experience should promote
- The ad, brief, screenshot, URL, Figma design, or template you want to start from
- The approved message, imagery, and brand guidance
- A clear idea of where the customer should go after clicking the main CTA

## 1. Open the right shop

Sign in to Jurni and confirm that you are working in the correct brand's shop. Your products, brand settings, integrations, experiences, and reports belong to that shop.

[Create or access your shop](/getting-started/create-account)

## 2. Create an experience

Start a new experience and give Jurni AI the context it needs. You can describe the page, attach campaign creative, reference a product, provide a screenshot, link an existing page, or begin from a template.

A useful first prompt explains:

- What you are promoting
- Who the campaign is for
- The offer or main message
- The page you want to create
- The action the customer should take

<Card title="Build your first page with AI" icon="sparkles" href="/build/ai-campaign-workflow">
  Follow the complete building workflow and use a copy-ready starting prompt.
</Card>

## 3. Connect the real customer action

Confirm that the experience uses the correct product, variant, selling plan, form, link, cart action, or checkout path. Visual text such as a price or discount does not configure the underlying commerce behavior.

[Products and Buy Boxes](/building-funnels/buy-box-overview) · [CTA actions](/build/cta-actions)

## 4. Review the experience

Check the full page on desktop and mobile. Review the message, imagery, offer, product selection, buttons, forms, cart behavior, and checkout handoff.

Use the [pre-launch QA checklist](/building-funnels/qa-checklist) for a complete review.

## 5. Publish and test the live URL

Publish using the route configured for your shop. Then open the exact URL your campaign audience will use and complete the intended customer journey yourself.

Do not rely only on the editor preview—the live URL is where you should verify products, actions, integrations, tracking, and responsive behavior.

[Choose a publishing method](/publishing/overview)

## 6. Send traffic and improve

Once the live experience and measurement are working, add the URL to your campaign. Use a [Smart Link](/building-funnels/smart-links) when you want to control the destination centrally or run a test.

After traffic arrives, review the experience results and decide what to improve next.
