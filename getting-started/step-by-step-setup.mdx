**Account Set Up: **

### **Core App set up: Settings> [<u>Account</u>](https://app.getjurni.ai/settings)**

- [**<u>Jurni Core App</u>**](https://app.getjurni.ai/settings#:~:text=owletcare.myshopify.com-,Activate%20Jurni%20Core,-Activate%20Jurni%20Core): Blocker to go live
  - Needs: Shopify Access to install 
  - Click **Activate & Open** to install via Shopify, activate in theme editor and click save
- [**<u>Subdomain setup</u>**](https://app.getjurni.ai/settings#:~:text=Enable%20Shopify%20Pages-,Subdomain%20Setup,-Connect%20a%20custom): Recommended 
  - Choose Your Subdomain DNS can take up to 24–48 hours; must be live before tracking/pixels are configured
- [**<u>Shopify Pages integration</u>**](https://app.getjurni.ai/settings#:~:text=Activate%20%26%20Open-,Shopify%20Pages,-Disabled): Optional (Dependent on customer)
  - Needs: Shopify Access to install
  - Click **Enable Shopify Pages **
  - Follow [<u>instructions on our Knowledge Base for next steps</u>](https://help.getjurni.ai/collections/8188601983-shopify_pages)

---

### **Members: Settings > [<u>Members</u>](https://app.getjurni.ai/settings/members)**

- Click **Invite Member**
- Choose Email Address, Choose Role, Send Invitation 
  - Admin: Manage shop and invite team members 
  - Staff: Manage shop dashboard and content
  - Designer: Design and manage experiences

---

### **Brand: Settings> [<u>Brand</u>](https://app.getjurni.ai/settings/brand) : All of the below is a blocker to building pages **

- Brand Logo URL:
  - Paste URL, click **Save Changes**
- Brand Colors: 
  - Click on each color to input color Hexcode or the dropper to choose the color on screen, **click Save Changed**
- Border Radius: 
  - Choose the pixel status for each card, **Save Changes **
- Typography:
  - Review and configure fonts for both Desktop and Mobile, slick **Save Changes**

---

### **AI Knowledge: Settings> [<u>AI Knowledge</u>](https://app.getjurni.ai/settings/agent)**

- Brand Guidance: Provide instructions, click **Save Guidance**
- Skills: 
  - Upload Skills, choose file, click **Open**
  - Click **Add Skill,** fill out Name, Description and Content, click **Add Skill **

---

- For any custom script, please discuss with our team.
