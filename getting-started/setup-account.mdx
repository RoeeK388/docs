This guide walks you through the core account setup steps in Jurni. You will configure a custom subdomain, connect third-party tracking pixels, invite team members, and define your brand and design system. Complete each section before generating your first agentic shopping journey.

## Custom Subdomain Setup

Your Jurni journeys are served from a subdomain you control. Set it up in **Settings > Account Settings > Account**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Subdomain Setup">
    Go to **Settings > Account Settings > Account**, then scroll to the Subdomain Setup section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your subdomain prefix">
    Type your preferred prefix, such as `shop`, `get`, `buy`, or any word that fits your brand. A live preview renders as you type so you can confirm the result before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and copy DNS values">
    Click **Save**. Jurni displays DNS values to copy into your DNS provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify propagation">
    Status shows **Pending** while DNS propagates. Click **Refresh** after a few minutes. Once propagation is complete, the status updates to **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  DNS propagation typically takes a few minutes but can take longer. Status will not update until propagation is complete.
</Note>

## Third-Party Tracking Pixels

Connect analytics and ad platforms in **Settings > Integrations > Data Integrations**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data Integrations">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations**, scroll to **Data Integrations**, and view the full list of available pixels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable a pixel">
    Toggle the pixel on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Pixel ID">
    Enter the Pixel ID or Tag ID from your platform's settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The pixel becomes active immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Repeat these steps for each pixel you want to install. Each integration is independent.

## App Integrations

Connect additional apps in the same **Data Integrations** section.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data Integrations">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations**, scroll to **Data Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable an app">
    Toggle the app on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your App Key">
    Enter the App Key or credential from your app's settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Inviting Team Members

Add collaborators in **Settings > Members**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invite flow">
    Click **Invite Member** in the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter email and role">
    Enter the team member's email and select their role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invitation">
    Click **Send Invitation**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only **Owner** and **Admin** roles can invite members. Available roles are:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Owner" icon="crown">
    Full control of the shop and organization.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Admin" icon="shield">
    Manage the shop and invite team members.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Staff" icon="user">
    Manage the shop dashboard and content.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Designer" icon="palette">
    Design and manage experiences.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Brand and Design System

Brand settings are the foundation Jurni uses when generating every agentic shopping journey. Configure them in **Settings > Brand**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set your logo">
    Enter a **Logo URL** by uploading an image or pasting a URL. The logo appears across all journeys.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your brand">
    Fill in **Brand Description** to define tone and style. The more specific your description, the better the generated output.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose brand colors">
    Click color swatches and enter hex values, or use the eyedropper to sample colors from a live site. Work through each token from surface to neutral, then click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust border radius">
    Set **Border Radius** defaults: Card is Small, Button is Medium, and Media is None. Adjust to match your design system and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure typography">
    Set mobile and desktop typography separately. Both are required even if the values are identical. Under **Headings**, select a Google Font, then adjust size, weight, text transform, line height, and letter spacing. Custom fonts can be uploaded but offer fewer adjustable properties.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Click **Save Changes** after each section within Brand settings. Changes are not auto-saved.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If you plan to use custom fonts, upload them before configuring typography so you can select them in the font dropdown.
</Tip>
